# Solid-state saddle audit

Audited 2026-11-17. The repository SHAs below are the revisions read for
this audit. The implementation target is the canonical stack, not a second
potential, optimizer, structure, or rate implementation in `rgsaddle`.

## Result

Most of the requested search mechanics already exist in `rgsaddle`.
Variable-cell Vineyard analysis and production validation through the
canonical potential path are missing. The pinned eOn revision already has
SS-NEB; it is not a missing feature.

## Existing `rgsaddle` mechanics

### Generalized coordinate and stress force

`src/solid_dimer.rs` is the canonical joint chart for searches. It stores

```text
q = (r0, J epsilon_free)
h = h0 (I + epsilon)
r = r0 (I + epsilon)
J = V0^(1/3) N^(1/6)
```

because `(V0 / N)^(1/3) sqrt(N) = V0^(1/3) N^(1/6)`.
`JointChart::gradient` pairs the atomic gradient with the stress-derived
cell gradient. `PointSurface::cauchy_stress` supplies analytic stress and
`finite_difference_cauchy_stress` is the explicit fallback.

`src/solid_state.rs` also contains the absolute-cell band formulation from
the 2012 SS-NEB work. Its mean-endpoint Jacobian, joint displacement,
stress force, enthalpy, and Cartesian cell step support `BandConfig::solid`.
This is a separate chart from `JointChart`; new dimer, joint SS-NEB, and
joint Vineyard work should share `JointChart`.

### SS-dimer

`src/solid_dimer.rs` implements rotation and translation by adapting a
`PointSurface` to `JointChart`. It supports a lower-triangular six-strain
mask, external stress, analytic or checked finite-difference stress,
projected index certification, and conversion back to Cartesian positions
and a cell. It cites Xiao, Sheppard, Rogal, and Henkelman, J. Chem. Phys.
140, 174104 (2014), DOI `10.1063/1.4873437`.

### SS-NEB

`src/ssneb.rs` implements climbing-image NEB directly on the same
`JointChart`. It uses joint tangents, joint springs, enthalpy ordering,
stress-coupled gradients, and the ordinary fixed-cell `BandSession` when
the cell is frozen. It cites Sheppard, Xiao, Chemelewski, Johnson, and
Henkelman, J. Chem. Phys. 136, 074103 (2012), DOI
`10.1063/1.3684549`.

The older `BandConfig::solid` path also implements the 2012 absolute-cell
formulation. Both are tested. `SolidNeb` is the correct owner for work that
must agree exactly with `SolidDimer`.

### Existing prefactor

`strain_rate::vineyard_prefactor` evaluates a stable logarithmic product
of positive Cartesian Hessian eigenvalues after translational modes and
the saddle's unstable mode have been removed. `sparse_hessian` can recover
an atomic Cartesian Hessian. Neither API includes strain degrees of
freedom or states the mass/metric convention for a mixed atom-strain
Hessian. Reusing the Cartesian function silently would therefore be
dimensionally wrong.

The missing library unit is a joint-chart Hessian at a stationary minimum
or index-1 saddle, its explicit excluded-mode policy, and a Vineyard ratio
whose atom and strain coordinates use one documented kinetic metric.
Finite-difference displacement and eigenspectrum tolerances must be caller
inputs or derived from supplied error bounds; they must not be hidden
constants.

## Validation audit

The repository has useful mechanics tests:

- `validation/ssneb_jacobian.py` derives the Jacobian and stress pairing.
- `validation/ssneb_bain_cell.py` derives the Bain cell parameterization
  and supercell scaling.
- `validation/bain_lj_reference.py` and `tests/solid_bain_lj.rs` check a
  switched Lennard-Jones Bain path.
- `tests/ssneb_validate_cu.rs` compares SS-dimer and SS-NEB on a Cu
  dumbbell and records an applied-shear series.

They do not complete the requested production validation. In particular,
`tests/ssneb_validate_cu.rs` contains its own Mishin EAM equations and is
not LAMMPS through `rgpot`. The historical
`benchmarks/ssneb_size.csv` was removed from the current tree and mixed
converged and unconverged rows; it is not evidence of thermodynamic-size
convergence. The Bain test is a mathematical reference, not one of the
published pressure-induced transformations in the 2012 SS-NEB paper.

The required validation must therefore add:

1. Cu vacancy and SIA dumbbell hops at zero pressure, with fixed and
   variable cells across increasing supercells, all convergence states
   recorded.
2. Barrier and joint Vineyard prefactor versus applied strain.
3. A case and observable taken from the 2012 paper, with the exact
   figure/table and units cited.
4. Commands, pinned revisions, wall times, raw finite values, and
   tolerances justified from discretization or requested solver accuracy.

## Patch ownership

The generic missing numerical API belongs in `rgsaddle`. `rgpot` remains
the potential and stress provider, `rgmin` remains the optimizer,
`readcon-core` remains structure I/O, and Amsel remains the rate consumer.
No owning-crate patch outside `rgsaddle` is justified by this audit.
