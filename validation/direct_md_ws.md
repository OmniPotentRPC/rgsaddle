# Direct-MD vacancy-SIA volume, Mishin Cu

Independent NVT trajectories of one vacancy and one <100> dumbbell on the Mishin EAM1 evaluated by `tests/mishin_cu.rs`. The driver is `examples/langevin_nvt.rs`. The cell is 13×13×13 conventional cells (8788 atoms) at the branch's NPT lattice constant. Capture is Wigner-Seitz occupancy (`MD_CAPTURE=ws`): every atom is assigned to its nearest lattice site after the drift of the cell's centre of mass is removed, and the pair has recombined at the first sample, every 0.25 ps, where every site holds exactly one atom. The columns `initial_ws_defects` and `final_ws_defects` count sites that do not. The 12-fold coordination test of Mwungeli, Liu, and Demkowicz (Phys. Rev. Materials 9, 023601 (2025)) cannot be used at 700 K, where thermal vibration alone keeps 100 to 200 atoms of the 13³ cell off 12-fold coordination, so its count never reaches zero. The shell sum counts recombined sites, with each orientation owning one third of its shell. The survival-curve volume defined in section 2 of that paper is computed from the same trajectories.

The clock starts at the ideal dumbbell (offset 1.025 Å) with Maxwellian velocities. There is no separate NPH equilibration. The thermostat is the Langevin velocity Verlet in `npt_lattice`, without the barostat. Friction and timestep are the columns `gamma_per_ps` and `dt_fs`.

Binomial standard error of a capture probability is sqrt(p (1-p) / N). The volume error adds the orientation contributions in quadrature. A shell whose trajectories all agree has a binomial error of zero.

## Capture fraction against time

The fraction is the number of trajectories of that shell and orientation that had recombined by that time, divided by the number of trajectories. `V_shell` is the shell sum, sites/3 times the fraction, over every shell that has all three orientations. Sites outside 10.8 Å are not in `V_shell`.

The first method of Mwungeli, Liu, and Demkowicz fits ln(n(t)/N) against time through the origin. The slope is -k/V_model, with V_model the 13³ cell volume. n(t)/N here counts every lattice site: a simulated shell contributes its capture fraction, and a site outside 10.8 Å is counted as not yet recombined. The recombination volume is k a² / (α D_i) with α = 2/3, divided by Ω = a³/4.
