# Direct-MD vacancy-SIA volume, Mishin Cu

Independent NVT trajectories of one vacancy and one <100> dumbbell on the Mishin EAM1 evaluated by `tests/mishin_cu.rs`. The driver is `examples/langevin_nvt.rs`. The cell is 13×13×13 conventional cells (8788 atoms) at the branch's NPT lattice constant. Capture is the paper's 12-fold test: coordination cutoff 3.1 Å, sampled every 0.25 ps (Mwungeli, Liu, and Demkowicz, Phys. Rev. Materials 9, 023601 (2025)). The shell sum counts recombined sites, with each orientation owning one third of its shell. The survival-curve volume defined in section 2 of that paper is computed from the same trajectories.

The clock starts at the ideal dumbbell (offset 1.025 Å) with Maxwellian velocities. There is no separate NPH equilibration. The thermostat is the Langevin velocity Verlet in `npt_lattice`, without the barostat. Friction and timestep are the columns `gamma_per_ps` and `dt_fs`.

A 3 ps control at 500 K, 2 fs, and gamma = 1/ps, sampled every 0.25 ps, fixes what a zero in the 12-fold count means. The perfect 13³ crystal started at 0 atoms off 12-fold coordination and then fluctuated: minimum 0, mean 6.2, maximum 14, with 4 of 12 samples exactly 0. The nearest shell, a <100> dumbbell, started at 13 and fell into that same cloud (minimum 0, mean 5.7, maximum 16, 2 of 12 samples exactly 0). The outermost shell inside 10.8 Å started at 20 and stayed separated (minimum 22, mean 32.0, maximum 47, 0 of 12 samples exactly 0). Capture in the table is the first sample whose off-12 count is 0.

Binomial standard error of a capture probability is sqrt(p (1-p) / N). The volume error adds the orientation contributions in quadrature. A shell whose trajectories all agree has a binomial error of zero.

## Capture fraction against time

The fraction is the number of trajectories of that shell and orientation that had recombined by that time, divided by the number of trajectories. `V_shell` is the shell sum, sites/3 times the fraction, over every shell that has all three orientations. Sites outside 10.8 Å are not in `V_shell`.

The first method of Mwungeli, Liu, and Demkowicz fits ln(n(t)/N) against time through the origin. The slope is -k/V_model, with V_model the 13³ cell volume. n(t)/N here counts every lattice site: a simulated shell contributes its capture fraction, and a site outside 10.8 Å is counted as not yet recombined. The recombination volume is k a² / (α D_i) with α = 2/3, divided by Ω = a³/4.

Fit on 11 samples up to 300 ps: slope -1.825953e-4 ± 3.297977e-5 /ps, k 1.916166e1 ± 3.460917e0 Å³/ps.

Table 1 quotes the Ref. [12] SIA diffusivity as 0.143 ± 0.006 Å²/ps at this temperature. First-method V_iv = 221.59 ± 41.09 Ω.

The first method of Mwungeli, Liu, and Demkowicz fits ln(n(t)/N) against time through the origin. The slope is -k/V_model, with V_model the 13³ cell volume. n(t)/N here counts every lattice site: a simulated shell contributes its capture fraction, and a site outside 10.8 Å is counted as not yet recombined. The recombination volume is k a² / (α D_i) with α = 2/3, divided by Ω = a³/4.
