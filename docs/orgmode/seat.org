#+title: In the seat
#+options: toc:nil num:nil

This crate is saddle mechanics, not a memory habitat. The seat that sits on a ticket is [[https://leidarljos.github.io/docs/sit/][ljos]]. rgmin is the minimizer. rgpot is the potential RPC. rgsaddle is the band, the inverted mode, and the index-1 Newton step.

* The name
[[https://leidarljos.github.io/docs/about/][leiðarljós]] is Icelandic for guiding light. =ljos= is the CLI.
