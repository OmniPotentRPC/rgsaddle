* C ABI (feature =capi=)
=include/rgsaddle.h=. =RGSADDLE_ABI_MAJOR= is 1. Status codes include =RGSADDLE_OK=, =RGSADDLE_SHAPE=, =RGSADDLE_SURFACE_FAILED=, =RGSADDLE_ABI_MISMATCH=. Methods on the wire: FIRE, L-BFGS, and quick-min. Min-mode kinds: dimer and Lanczos. Index-1 entries: =rgsaddle_nichols_step=, =rgsaddle_prfo_step=, =rgsaddle_hessian_powell=, =rgsaddle_hessian_bofill=, =rgsaddle_cap_max_abs=, and =rgsaddle_index1_step=. Band cache entries: =rgsaddle_band_restart=, =rgsaddle_band_evaluation= (=RGSADDLE_NO_EVALUATION= when nothing is cached at the band's positions). Min-mode reuse: =rgsaddle_minmode_set_position=, =rgsaddle_minmode_set_mode=, =rgsaddle_minmode_estimate=. A Lanczos estimate advances one Hessian action and keeps the Krylov basis until =set_position=, =set_mode=, or =reset=; the basis grows to the free dimension, the mode after k actions lies in the k + 1 Lanczos vectors (its curvature is the Ritz value of the k), and a dimer estimate is the full rotation. Dimer Polak-Ribiere history survives =set_position= and is cleared by =set_mode= and =reset=; a failed callback leaves it unchanged. =rgsaddle_minmode_config_t.difference= picks =RGSADDLE_DIFFERENCE_FORWARD= or =_CENTRAL=, and =rotation_angle_tol= stops a dimer rotation whose optimal angle falls under it (radians; the Rust default is five degrees, 0 off, and a C config stores the value it is given). Both fields are read only from a config whose version minor is 5 or newer. =RGSADDLE_ABI_MINOR= is 21. =rgsaddle_idpp_path= writes the image-dependent pair potential initial path, endpoints included, and takes a row-major cell or NULL. =rgsaddle_ocineb_step= reports the hand-off phase (band, tangent alignment, or minimum-mode translation). =rgsaddle_connect= runs that search to completion: the host passes two endpoint arrays and a surface that writes energy and the Cartesian gradient for one image; =rgsaddle_connect_report_t= returns energy, the Euclidean gradient norm, kind (=s=, =u=, or =f=), the Hessian index, and the evaluation count. Finite differences that decide a step or an index are included; the Lanczos acceptance does not build a dense Hessian. =RGSADDLE_BAND_CI_AFTER= (band flag bit 2) arms the climbing image under =ci_trigger_force= or =ci_trigger_factor= times the first force, either of which may be 0; =RGSADDLE_BAND_NO_MIN_MODE= (band flag bit 3, minor 21) is accepted and not read: a band from =rgsaddle_band_create= is CI-NEB, and the min-mode refinement of the climbing image is OCI-NEB, =rgsaddle_ocineb_create=; =RGSADDLE_STALLED= reports a band step that left the band unchanged above =force_tol=. =RGSADDLE_METHOD_QUICKMIN= runs quick-min on the band. =rgsaddle_band_set_quickmin_variant= selects =RGSADDLE_QUICKMIN_RGMIN=, =_EON= or =_PER_ATOM=. A min-mode session returns null for that code. =rgsaddle_band_create_solid= builds a solid-state band; =rgsaddle_band_step_solid= steps it on an =rgsaddle_solid_surface_fn= whose =rgsaddle_solid_surface_request_t= carries cells in and Cauchy stresses out; =rgsaddle_band_cells= and =rgsaddle_band_solid_jacobian= read it back. The report's =evaluations= field took the place of =reserved= at minor 5. =cargo cinstall= installs =include/rgsaddle.h= as written.

* Python package
The importable package is =rgsaddle=. =rgsaddle._core= is the nanobind extension. =import rgsaddle= does not import ASE.

=SurfaceAdapter= keeps =get_forces(apply_constraint=False)= on =raw_forces=. The returned gradient is minus =get_forces()= after constraints. The adapter evaluates the given rows in order on the calling thread. =owners()= and =reentrant()= are what a driver uses to group. A second call while the first holds the adapter raises =RuntimeError=.

* Tests that pin the contract
- =tests/ocineb.rs= — the hand-off waits for a stable climbing image, restores it when the curvature is positive, and reaches the same double-well saddle as a climbing-image band. =tests/ocineb_abi.rs= runs =tests/c/ocineb_abi.c=
- =tests/connect_driver.rs= — two-barrier toy, the C ABI, LJ38 pair 0 and pair 39, and ignored release samples of the 51-pair connect score and the CI-NEB templates on 20 pairs
- =tests/double_well.rs= — band finds the origin saddle, endpoints pinned
- =tests/autoneb.rs= — ASE AutoNEB decisions and the Müller-Brown insertion sequence. =tests/autoneb_gate.rs= hashes the insertion list and the trajectory at 1, 2, 3 and 8 threads, and an ignored release run compares AutoNEB with CI-NEB at one force-call budget. Strong scaling of a full AutoNEB run is an ignored test in the crate.
- =tests/minmode_saddle.rs= — dimer / Lanczos
- =tests/nichols_saddle.rs= — Nichols step against i-PI on the same Hessian and gradient, Muller-Brown saddles, quadratic saddle
- =tests/prfo_saddle.rs= — partitioned RFO against the augmented Hessian, trust sphere changes the mode ratio
- =tests/c_abi.rs= — header and symbols with =capi=
- =tests/solid_bain_lj.rs= — =tests/c/solid_bain_lj.c= runs the solid-state band from bcc to fcc for switched LJ on the two-atom tetragonal cell through =rgsaddle_band_create_solid=; the climbing image matches the relaxed scan of =validation/bain_lj_reference.py= in c/a within 2.5e-4 and in barrier within 1.2e-6 eV/atom, and every image keeps a diagonal tetragonal cell and its fractional coordinates
- =validation/= — sympy and sollya checks of the dimer rotation, the Lanczos residual bound, and the finite-difference curvature; =bain_lj_reference.py= (numpy, scipy) scans the relaxed Bain path that =tests/c/solid_bain_lj.c= checks against, and =ssneb_bain_cell.py= (sympy) checks its strain parametrisation and Jacobian
