#+TITLE: PAFI types
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil author:nil

Full API documentation for every item below is on [[https://docs.rs/rgsaddle][docs.rs/rgsaddle]], under =rgsaddle::pafi=; =cargo doc --open= builds the same pages from a checkout. This page is the inventory: every public configuration and result type the module exports, and the functions that connect them. =tutorials/pafi= walks through using them; =explanation/pafi-ohtst= has the theory.

* Path types
| Type | Role |
|------+------|
| =ArcPath= | A cubic-spline path through ordered frames. =from_positions= builds it from a list of Cartesian configurations, reactant first, parameterized by the summed chord lengths from the first; =new= takes frames with a parameter of the caller's; =reversed= is the same curve run from the other end; =bounds= gives the parameter of the first and last frame, =arcs= every frame's parameter, and =geometry(arc)= the position, tangent, curvature and jerk of the spline at a parameter inside =bounds=, and =SaddleError::PafiArcOutsidePath= outside them |
| =PathFrame= | One frame of a path: =positions= (the Cartesian configuration) and =arc= (its path parameter; the chord length from the first frame for =from_positions=) |
| =PathGeometry= | =positions= (=X(s)=), =tangent= (=X'=), =curvature= (=X''=), =jerk= (=X'''=, constant on each spline segment) and =arc= (=s=) at one point along a path |
| =FixedEnergyTrajectory= | Mass-weighted coordinate spline and parallel-force spline (=SplineEnds::Natural=) on the normalized polyline arc length in =[0, 1]=. =from_gradients= builds both splines from image positions, gradients, and coordinate masses; =from_images= evaluates a =BandSurface= once per image in order; =coordinate= and =parallel_force= return the two =Spline= curves; =acceleration= evaluates the normalized-arc acceleration on the inverted potential; =half= integrates from the left turning point until the path reaches =1.0= or the velocity turns back; =period= returns twice the duration of =half= |
| =TrajectoryPoint= | One sample of inverted-potential motion along the normalized path from =FixedEnergyTrajectory::half=: =time= from the left turning point, normalized =arc=, and =velocity= (=d(arc)/dt=) |

* Sampling configuration and results
| Type | Role |
|------+------|
| =PafiConfig= | Non-exhaustive; chain =with_*= setters from =default=. Defaults: =thermal = 0.02585= (300 K in eV), =mobility = 1.0=, =time_step = 1e-3=, =integrator = PafiIntegrator::LeimkuhlerMatthews=, =mean_force = PafiMeanForce::ReferenceCode=, =thermalize = 200=, =stride = 5=, =samples = 200=, =blocks = 5=, =trajectories = 1=, =seed = 1=, =fix_com = true=, =com_atoms = None=, =retain = 16=, =target_error = None=, =max_rounds = 4=, =region = None=, =max_jump = Some(0.4)=, =quench = RelaxConfig::default().with_method(Method::Ode12r)=. =thermal= is =kT= in the surface's energy unit; =mobility= and =time_step= set the overdamped Langevin step; =integrator= picks =PafiIntegrator= and =mean_force= picks =PafiMeanForce=; =thermalize= is the minimum warm-up before the automated equilibration test, =stride=, =samples= and =blocks= control the production run and its block standard error, and =trajectories= runs that many independent trajectories per plane; =seed= fixes the noise stream; =fix_com= removes the mean Cartesian drift when the surface certifies =CartesianLayout::Cartesian= or when =com_atoms= (=with_com_atoms=) supplies the real atom count for =CartesianLayout::Unknown=, while rejecting =CartesianLayout::Wrapped= and =CartesianLayout::NonCartesian=; =retain= keeps that many evenly spaced recorded configurations on each plane for later reuse; =region= restricts the walk to a hard =Region=; =max_jump= and =quench= (a =RelaxConfig=) reject a stretch of the walk whose in-plane minimum has moved more than =max_jump= from the path's own, so the walk stays in the basin of the path; =target_error= and =max_rounds= run extra sampling rounds on every plane, inverse-variance combined with the first, until the barrier error meets the target or the round budget is spent (=howto/pafi-ohtst-restarting=) |
| =PafiIntegrator= | =LeimkuhlerMatthews= (the default, second order for stationary averages) or =EulerMaruyama= (the direct step of the paper's Eq. (9)) |
| =PafiMeanForce= | =ReferenceCode= (the default: the first term of the paper's Eq. (8), as LAMMPS =fix pafi= averages it) or =PublishedEquation8= (both terms, the second being =kT d/ds ln(abs(psi) / norm(X'))=) |
| =HyperplaneAverage= | One plane's result: =gradient= (=dF/ds=), its =error=, the mean =psi= (the correction term, zero on a straight path), =samples= recorded (summed over rounds), the integrated autocorrelation time =tau= in samples, =n_eff= (=(samples - discard) / (2 tau)=), total force =evaluations=, the =retained= configurations on the plane, the =equilibration= report and the number of stretches =rejected= for leaving the basin (=howto/pafi-ohtst-checking-convergence= covers reading =tau= and =n_eff=) |
| =FreeEnergyProfile= | The potential of mean force integrated from the first plane: per-plane =arc=, =gradient= and =gradient_error=, the trapezoid-integrated =free_energy= and its propagated =free_energy_error=, the =barrier= (the profile's maximum, with the reactant held at zero) and its =barrier_error=, the index of that maximum in =dividing=, and total recorded =samples= across every plane and round |

* Functions
| Function | Role |
|----------+------|
| =average_hyperplane= | Samples one hyperplane by overdamped Langevin dynamics restricted to the plane, with =PafiConfig::trajectories= trajectories, and returns its =HyperplaneAverage= |
| =average_hyperplane_with_sampler= | The same estimator over any =HyperplaneSampler=, such as a molecular-dynamics engine |
| =pafi_profile= | Samples every arc length in parallel on rayon's global thread pool and integrates to a =FreeEnergyProfile=, returning it alongside every plane's =HyperplaneAverage=; runs extra rounds when =PafiConfig::target_error= is set |
| =integrate_profile= | Trapezoid-integrates a set of arc lengths, gradients and their errors directly, with no sampling, into a =FreeEnergyProfile= |
| =zero_temperature_barrier= | Integrates the potential's own gradient along a path with no sampling (=PafiConfig::thermal= plays no part): a check of the path itself, exact on a converged minimum-energy path |
| =arc_at_time= | Cubic Hermite interpolation of the normalized arc coordinate at =time= on a half-trajectory slice =&[TrajectoryPoint]= using the endpoint arcs and velocities of the bracketing interval |
| =attempt_frequency= | Not part of =rgsaddle::pafi= itself (it is exported from =rgsaddle::defect_pair=), but the usual prefactor for turning a PAFI barrier into a rate: =nu = angular * sqrt(lambda / mass) / (2 pi)= from a curvature =lambda= along the path tangent at the reactant. =rgsaddle::arrhenius= (from =rgsaddle::strain_rate=, fixed to eV and kelvin) is the wrong call for a reduced-units surface; multiply =nu= by =exp(-barrier / kT)= directly instead, as =tutorials/pafi= does |

* Ring-polymer centroid PAFI (=rgsaddle::ring=)
| Item | Role |
|------+------|
| =RingSettings= | Non-exhaustive settings for a ring polymer: =beta= (=with_beta=), =hbar= (=with_hbar=), and per-coordinate =masses= (=with_masses=) of one bead |
| =RingPolymer= | Wraps =beads= copies of a =PointSurface= with the primitive Feynman and Hibbs spring as a single =PointSurface= (=CartesianLayout::Wrapped= for more than one bead). =action= evaluates =beta * U=; =time_step= bounds the overdamped step from the stiffest spring eigenvalue and a Gershgorin bound reconstructed from =PointSurface::hessian_vector= at every supplied physical image |
| =BeadSample= | One bead count in =bead_study=: =beads=, zero-step extrapolated =profile= (=FreeEnergyProfile=), =step_error= between the quarter-step and eighth-step barriers, conservative =effective_samples= and =tau=, and the caller's =time_step= |
| =RingCon= | Instanton orbit loaded under the =readcon= feature: concatenated bead coordinates in =orbit=, one-bead Cartesian =masses=, and =beads= |
| =centroid_hyperplane= | Builds a flat centroid =Hyperplane= through a =collapsed= physical image with the bead tangent repeated on every bead |
| =collapsed= | Repeats one physical bead geometry across =beads= in bead-major order |
| =centroid_path= | Repeats each image of a physical path onto every bead and builds an =ArcPath= |
| =bead_study= | Runs =pafi_profile= on the centroid path at each requested bead count using quarter-step and eighth-step refinements of =PafiConfig::time_step=, rejecting =fix_com = true=, temperature mismatches, or steps above =RingPolymer::time_step= |
| =orbit_from_con=, =ring_path_from_con= | Read one instanton orbit (=RingCon=) or a multi-orbit ring path (=(ArcPath, RingCon)=) from a =.con= file when the =readcon= feature is enabled |

* What the tests check
=tests/pafi_analytic.rs= checks the sampled profile on a one-dimensional barrier and on a two-dimensional well with a known transverse free-energy shift, against the closed form the shift gives. =tests/ring_pafi.rs= and =examples/centroid_pmf.rs= cover ring-polymer centroid PAFI sampling and =bead_study=. =examples/pafi_tutorial.rs= runs the same kind of check, on a different surface, with numbers printed rather than asserted; =tutorials/pafi= walks through it.
