- Breaking: a climbing band refines its climbing image by the lowest mode, the OCI-NEB of Goswami, Gunde, and Jónsson, Front. Chem. 14, 1807063 (2026), doi:10.3389/fchem.2026.1807063, Algorithm 1. ~BandConfig::min_mode~ is ~Some(MinModeRefine::default())~; ~None~ keeps the plain CI-NEB, and AutoNEB's windows keep it. Once the same image has been highest for three band steps and its largest per-atom force is under half the first evaluation's, a Lanczos estimate seeded with the band tangent (Olsen, Kroes, Henkelman, Arnaldsson, and Jónsson, J. Chem. Phys. 121, 9776 (2004), doi:10.1063/1.1809574; eight actions, relative tolerance 0.05) gives the mode, and that image climbs alone by the order-1 P-RFO step of Baker, J. Comput. Chem. 7, 385 (1986), doi:10.1002/jcc.540070402, on a dense TS-BFGS model with the trust schedule of Sella (Hermes, Sarsfield, and Zádor, J. Chem. Theory Comput. 18, 6974 (2022), doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.2c00395), bounded per atom by ~max_move~. The model starts at ~h_0 I~ with ~h_0~ one finite-difference curvature along the force perpendicular to the mode, and holds the Lanczos curvature on the mode with every other direction reflected to a minimum. Lanczos runs again when the force grows past 1.5 times its lowest value since the last estimate or the model's lowest mode overlaps the previous one by under 0.7. The image returns to the band when its mode overlaps the tangent by under 0.5, when the curvature turns positive, or after 1000 steps, at the aligned point of lowest force, and the trigger re-arms by eqs. 13 to 15. The band reports ~Converged~ when the climbing image's force is under ~force_tol~. The defaults are looser than the paper's ~λ_rel~ 0.31, ~κ~ 5 and ~α_tol~ 0.85 because the P-RFO climb follows its own model mode. ~BandReport::curvature~ carries the mode curvature on a refinement step, and ~BandSurface::eval_image~ (default: one row through ~eval~) evaluates the climbing image alone. C ABI minor 19 adds ~RGSADDLE_BAND_NO_MIN_MODE~ (band flag bit 3) for the old behaviour and fills ~rgsaddle_report_t.curvature~ on band steps; during a refinement a per-image host is asked for the climbing image with ~RGSADDLE_REQ_ONE_IMAGE~ and a batched host for one geometry with ~n_images = 1~ and ~image = -1~. The CON restart test's band now takes the refinement, so its file is 1605 bytes where it was 1602; the uninterrupted and resumed runs still match byte for byte.

- ~connect~ first climbs from the highest image of an IDPP band. Goswami, Gunde, and Jónsson, Front. Chem. 14 (2026), doi:10.3389/fchem.2026.1807063, climb the highest image; Goswami, arXiv:2603.14737, is the energy-weighted band this search is measured against. The initial path is the image-dependent pair potential of Smidstrup, Pedersen, Stokbro, and Jónsson, J. Chem. Phys. 140, 214106 (2014), doi:10.1063/1.4878664; that relaxation is not a surface call. The seed is the improved tangent of Henkelman and Jónsson, J. Chem. Phys. 113, 9978 (2000), doi:10.1063/1.1323224. The climb is Lanczos min-mode following (Malek and Mousseau, Phys. Rev. E 62, 7723 (2000), doi:10.1103/PhysRevE.62.7723; Olsen, Kroes, Henkelman, Arnaldsson, and Jónsson, J. Chem. Phys. 121, 9776 (2004), doi:10.1063/1.1809574) with relative tolerance 0.05 and at most eight actions, translated by L-BFGS (Liu and Nocedal, Math. Program. 45, 503 (1989), doi:10.1007/BF01589116) on the effective force of Henkelman and Jónsson, J. Chem. Phys. 111, 7010 (1999), doi:10.1063/1.480097. A segment is accepted when the Euclidean force is at most the tolerance, the tracked curvature is negative, and two Lanczos actions in the orthogonal complement are not clearly negative. The dense Hessian is not built for that acceptance. Otherwise the search is COMPASS (Sommer-Jörgensen and Goedecker, J. Chem. Phys. 160, 064110 (2024), doi:10.1063/5.0186903) with the SQNM update (Schaefer, Ghasemi, Roy, and Goedecker, J. Chem. Phys. 142, 034112 (2015), doi:10.1063/1.4905665). A split minimizes the new nodes before the next segment, in the sense of OPTIM NEWCONNECT (Carr, Trygubenko, and Wales, J. Chem. Phys. 122, 234903 (2005), doi:10.1063/1.1931587) and pele ~DoubleEndedConnect~. That chain's point is kept when a counted, rigid-projected Hessian has index 1. C ABI minor 18 adds ~rgsaddle_connect~. Python ~connect~ uses the same one-image energy-and-gradient callback and releases the GIL for the search.

- A solid-state band test with an answer known outside the band. The case is the Bain path from bcc to fcc of LJ (epsilon 1 eV, sigma 1 A), switched to zero over [2.0, 2.2] A, on the two-atom tetragonal cell. ~validation/bain_lj_reference.py~ scans the energy per atom over c/a on a 2.5e-4 grid with the volume relaxed. bcc and fcc are minima. The saddle sits at c/a 1.09331985, 0.0590356373 eV/atom above bcc, and has index 1 over the six cell strains. ~tests/c/solid_bain_lj.c~ runs ~rgsaddle_band_create_solid~ with FIRE, 7 interior images and the climbing image armed at 0.5. It converges to a largest component under 1e-4 in 446 iterations and 3131 surface rows, at c/a 1.09331984 and 0.0590356373 eV/atom. The saddle is unstable to the k = (1/2, 0, 1/2) shuffle. A band on a 5x5x4 supercell (200 atoms) reaches the tetragonal saddle and then leaves it once rounding seeds that mode. It converges on a lower path with a 0.0266 eV/atom barrier, so the scan is a reference for the two-atom cell and not for that supercell. Climbing from the first evaluation with ~max_move~ 0.1 drives the two-atom band into the compressed bct side, where E(c/a) has no maximum. With ~max_move~ 0.02 it converges in 327 iterations.

- ~sparse_hessian~ recovers a cutoff Hessian by a direct Curtis-Powell-Reid partition. ~distance2_colouring~ is greedy in smallest-last order. A block of colours is one batched surface call, sized by ~SparseHessianOpts::memory_budget~ rather than by the thread count, and the blocks are written back in colour order. Each geometry is its own chunk. ~from_positions~ builds the pattern with a linkcell cutoff walk plus ~search_skin~, which is ~max(2 * step, skin)~. ~lowest_eigenpairs~ is a thick-restart Lanczos with a residual stop, an iteration cap, and the residuals in the result. Its inner product is a fixed-order dot, serial below 65,536 elements. Powell and Toint (1979) cover the symmetric direct read and the substitution. Star colouring (Coleman and Moré 1984) stays a later option.
- AutoNEB stays in Rust on ~BandConfig::autoneb~. The C ABI stays at minor 16.
- AutoNEB records a trajectory only when ~record_samples~ is set. The default stores nothing and takes no sample lock. One stored call is one ~f64~ per degree of freedom.
- AutoNEB follows the ASE window and spring rescaling. The fill from two endpoints sets every segment to ~k * (n_simul + 1)~, and each insertion doubles the cut segment. The search stops after the climbing window unless ~full_band~ is set. On the double well that default used 1.42 times as many force calls as one fixed CI-NEB. Müller-Brown from B to C used 0.46 times as many, and the two-barrier path from A to B has mean ratio 0.95.
- AutoNEB reuses one band session while the window indices do not change, so the L-BFGS pairs survive the climb on that window. A new index set starts empty. Known endpoint energies are not evaluated again.
- AutoNEB image evaluation keeps one thread pool for the search. Each evaluation is a fixed set of contiguous blocks, stored by image index after every block has finished. One thread evaluates the batch in a single call. There is no SIMD path. The warm-step speedup is capped by ~n_simul~ free images.
- AutoNEB temporarily refuses a weighted or Onsager-Machlup spring. Zoom-NEB will recompute those springs when an image is inserted.
- AutoNEB grows a band from the supplied images. It inserts where the peak-weighted energy gap or the segment length is worst, relaxes that local window, and arms the climbing image on the last stage. The inserted image is the minimum-image midpoint.

- C ABI minor 16. ~RGSADDLE_METHOD_QUICKMIN~ runs quick-min on the band. ~rgsaddle_band_create_solid~ builds a solid-state band and ~rgsaddle_band_step_solid~ steps it: one ~rgsaddle_solid_surface_request_t~ carries each image's positions and cell in and its energy, gradient and Cauchy stress out. ~rgsaddle_band_cells~ and ~rgsaddle_band_solid_jacobian~ read the cells and the Jacobian back. ~BandConfig::quickmin~ selects the same step. A min-mode session returns null for that method code. FIRE stays the default.
- The band can carry the generalized solid-state cell block. The tangent acts on the atomic displacement plus the averaged, Jacobian-scaled strain, and the cell force is minus the cell volume times the Cauchy stress plus hydrostatic pressure, divided by the Jacobian. ~BandSurface::eval_cells~ supplies the stress. The Jacobian is fixed from the mean endpoint volume. ~SolidState~ is non-exhaustive: build it with ~SolidState::new~, ~with_pressure~ and ~with_weight~. ~validation/ssneb_jacobian.py~ derives the units, the strain-stress pairing and the rotation-free lower triangle.
- Minimum-mode translation follows the curvature. While the curvature along the mode is not negative the effective gradient is minus the parallel part and the perpendicular force is dropped. A negative curvature keeps the perpendicular force and reflects the parallel part. The dimer and Lanczos share that step. ~validation/minmode_switch.py~ derives both branches on a quadratic model, and the Lean ~MinMode~ contract states the switched force (~switchedForce~ and the ~switched_force_*~ theorems).
- C ABI minor 5. ~rgsaddle_band_restart~ restarts the band optimizer and keeps the endpoint energies and the last evaluation. ~rgsaddle_band_reset~ is the surface-change boundary and drops both. ~rgsaddle_band_evaluation~ returns the energies, gradients and projected force at the current band, or ~RGSADDLE_NO_EVALUATION~. The report's ~evaluations~ field takes the place of ~reserved~ and counts the surface rows of a step.
- C ABI minor 5, min-mode reuse. ~rgsaddle_minmode_set_position~ moves a session, with the host's gradient when it has one, ~rgsaddle_minmode_set_mode~ replaces the seed, and ~rgsaddle_minmode_estimate~ refreshes the lowest mode without a translation. ~rgsaddle_minmode_config_t~ gains ~rotation_angle_tol~ (radians; 0 turns the angle test off) and ~difference~ (~RGSADDLE_DIFFERENCE_FORWARD~ or ~_CENTRAL~), read only from configs whose version minor is 5 or newer; the Rust ~MinModeConfig~ carries the same fields.
