- ~ExpBandPrecon~ (~rgsaddle_band_set_exp_precon~) applies the exponential metric of Packwood et al., J. Chem. Phys. 144, 164109 (2016), inside the band projection as Eq. (9) and (10) of the same paper write it: each image is driven by ~-(P^-1 g - t_P (t_P . g)) + (k / mu) ((d+ - d-) . P t_P) t_P~ with ~t_P~ the tangent normalised in the P-norm, one matrix per interior image, rebuilt when an atom has moved ~r_nn / 2~, the energy scale ~mu~ estimated once on the first interior image. The band relaxes to the minimum energy path of the metric's geometry, where the gradient lies along ~P t_P~ and the Euclidean perpendicular force does not vanish; the band force that convergence and the report measure is the residual of Eq. (11), ~-(g - P t_P (t_P . g))~ per image. An armed climbing image reflects the preconditioned gradient in the P-metric, ~-(P^-1 g - 2 t_P (t_P . g))~, whose fixed point is the saddle. Only a uniform spring is accepted with the metric.

- A Lanczos ~rgsaddle_minmode_estimate~ (~MinModeSession::push_estimate~) returns, after k actions, a mode in the k + 1 Lanczos vectors. The last vector is the residual direction that the k-th action already produced. In the Lanczos basis an eigenvector's components are the Lanczos polynomials at its eigenvalue (Parlett, The Symmetric Eigenvalue Problem, SIAM (1998), doi:10.1137/1.9781611971163, Ch. 7), and they need only the k known rows of the tridiagonal. They are evaluated at an estimate of the lowest eigenvalue: the next Ritz value predicted with ~alpha_{k+1} = alpha_k~, extrapolated with the two previous Ritz values by Aitken's delta-squared (Aitken, Proc. R. Soc. Edinburgh 46, 289 (1927), doi:10.1017/S0370164600022070) when they fall with shrinking steps. At the Ritz value the formula is the old Ritz vector. No surface call is added, the reported curvature is still the Ritz value of the k vectors, and ~estimate_mode~ and ~step~ are unchanged. The dimer and Lanczos framing is Goswami, Gunde, and Jónsson, Front. Chem. 14 (2026), doi:10.3389/fchem.2026.1807063, and Goswami, arXiv:2603.10992; the Lanczos stop is Olsen, Kroes, Henkelman, Arnaldsson, and Jónsson, J. Chem. Phys. 121, 9776 (2004), doi:10.1063/1.1809574. Sella 0.1.1 (Hermes, Sargsyan, Najm, and Zádor, J. Chem. Theory Comput. 15, 6536 (2019), doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.9b00869) has no Hessian to precondition with on this task, so its Jacobi-Davidson expansion is the Lanczos residual. It tests the Ritz vector of the vectors it has applied the Hessian to, so it takes the same actions as the old estimate; its archived 17.8 is the centre gradient plus about 16.8 actions, without the final centre evaluation the harness adds. On the board geometries the best vector of the k vectors needs 15.895 actions on average (17.895 with those two calls), so no extraction from the k vectors passes 17.8. The estimate was chosen on three sets the board does not score, with the lowest eigenvector of a central-difference Hessian as the reference and overlap 0.99. - Breaking: the band refinement fits its climb model to a pair-stretch Hessian and the Lanczos block, follows its mode, and takes the climbing image once it has kept its index for one band step, at any force. The model is the pair-stretch Hessian of Lindh, Bernhardsson, Karlström, and Malmqvist, Chem. Phys. Lett. 241, 423 (1995), doi:10.1016/0009-2614(95)00646-L, with the exponential pair weight and diagonal stabilisation of Packwood et al., J. Chem. Phys. 144, 164109 (2016), doi:10.1063/1.4947024, scaled by the smallest ratio of measured to model curvature over the positive Lanczos directions, and every Lanczos action is then imposed exactly; it takes the place of the ~h_0 I~ start. ~MinModeSession::krylov_block~ returns those actions. With ~remove_translation~ the model, the step and the secant pairs live off the rigid translations, and off the rotations when there is no cell. Each P-RFO step maximizes along the model eigenvector closest to the mode followed a step before. Lanczos runs again once the followed model curvature is above ~curvature_retained~ (0.3) of the measured one or the mode turned under ~min_overlap~ (0.8), at most every ~refresh_interval~ (4) steps; ~refresh_factor~ is removed. The trust radius halves on an energy ratio outside ~[1/4, 4]~ and doubles on one inside ~(3/4, 4/3)~ from a step at the radius, in place of Sella's schedule. ~MinModeRefine~ defaults: ~trigger_factor~ infinite, ~stable_steps~ 1, ~alignment_tol~ 0.85 for the start, the new ~climb_alignment~ off, the new ~retry_factor~ 0.5 (a refinement that did not lower the force waits also for half the force it started from), ~krylov_dim~ 20. A refinement that leaves the image where it began keeps the band optimizer's history. ~OcinebConfig::refine~ turns ~climb_alignment~ on and ~retry_factor~ off, so ~OcinebSession~ is the same mechanism with the published exits. The band converges when the climbing image does, so after an early hand-off the other images stay where the band left them; a host that wants the relaxed path sets a finite ~trigger_factor~ or ~min_mode: None~. C ABI minor 23 adds ~RGSADDLE_REQ_CLIMBING_IMAGE~ (request flags bit 1): from a band config at minor 23 the refinement's one-image requests set it, and a batched request carries the climbing image's band index in ~image~ in place of -1. ~rgsaddle_ocineb_step~ keeps its point surface request.

- Breaking: a climbing band refines its climbing image by the lowest mode, the OCI-NEB of Goswami, Gunde, and Jónsson, Front. Chem. 14, 1807063 (2026), doi:10.3389/fchem.2026.1807063, Algorithm 1. ~BandConfig::min_mode~ is ~Some(MinModeRefine::default())~; ~None~ keeps the plain CI-NEB, and AutoNEB's windows keep it. Chem. 7, 385 (1986), doi:10.1002/jcc.540070402, on a dense TS-BFGS model with the trust schedule of Sella (Hermes, Sarsfield, and Zádor, J. Chem. The model starts at ~h_0 I~ with ~h_0~ one finite-difference curvature along the force perpendicular to the mode, and holds the Lanczos curvature on the mode with every other direction reflected to a minimum. Lanczos runs again when the force grows past 1.5 times its lowest value since the last estimate or the model's lowest mode overlaps the previous one by under 0.7. The image returns to the band when its mode overlaps the tangent by under 0.3, when the curvature turns positive, or after 1000 steps, at the aligned point of lowest force, and the trigger re-arms by eqs. 13 to 15. The band reports ~Converged~ when the climbing image's force is under ~force_tol~. The defaults are looser than the paper's ~λ_rel~ 0.31, ~κ~ 5 and ~α_tol~ 0.85 because the P-RFO climb follows its own model mode. ~BandReport::curvature~ carries the mode curvature on a refinement step, and ~BandSurface::eval_image~ (default: one row through ~eval~) evaluates the climbing image alone.
