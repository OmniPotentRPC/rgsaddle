# towncrier release notes start

* Unreleased

** Added

- Fixed-energy line-integral NEB (LI-NEB): host-stepped session controls, discrete fixed-energy action and action-force helpers, natural cubic interpolation support, and fixed-energy MEP seeding are now available in Rust, C, and Python.
  The C ABI minor is 42 via this LI-NEB addition.

* 0.5.0 (2026-11-17)

** Breaking

- Every public configuration struct outside the C ABI, except ~DirectSolver~, is ~#[non_exhaustive]~.
  ~AutoNebTuning~, ~BandConfig~, ~CiConfig~, ~ExpBandPrecon~, ~ForceMatchOpts~, ~GpuPolicy~, ~IdppConfig~, ~IndexKConfig~, ~InternalWeights~, ~IrcConfig~, ~KappaDimerConfig~, ~MinModeConfig~, ~OcinebConfig~, ~RtrConfig~, ~SamdConfig~, ~SellaMinConfig~, ~SellaSaddleConfig~, ~SidppConfig~, ~TrustSchedule~ and ~VelocitySofteningConfig~ join the configs that already were, so a struct literal of one, including one that ends in ~..Default::default()~, or an exhaustive pattern of one no longer compiles outside this crate.
  ~CurvatureBound~, ~DefectPairConfig~, ~FmmSolver~, ~PafiConfig~, ~ShootingConfig~ and ~TrustPolicy~, new in this release, are non-exhaustive too.
  The fields stay public: reading one, or assigning to one of a value already built, still compiles.
  Each config has a ~#[must_use]~ ~with_*~ setter for every public field, and each setter this change adds for an ~Option~ field takes the value or ~None~.
  ~ConnectConfig~, ~MinModeRefine~ and ~ZoomConfig~, already non-exhaustive, gain a setter for every field, and ~SolidState~ gains ~with_cells~.
  Start from ~Default~ or the named constructor and chain the setters: ~BandConfig { force_tol: 1e-3, ..BandConfig::default() }~ becomes ~BandConfig::default().with_force_tol(1e-3)~, and a nested write such as ~config.band.max_move = 0.05~, which still compiles, can also be written ~let band = config.band.clone().with_max_move(0.05);~ then ~let config = config.with_band(band);~.
  ~ConnectConfig~ gains ~Default~, which is ~ConnectConfig::optbench(250_000)~, the ~max_calls~ default of the Python ~connect~, and ~TrustSchedule~ gains ~Default~, which is ~TrustSchedule::minimum()~; ~CurvatureBound::new~ takes ~lambda0~, ~slope~ and ~fraction~.
  No existing default changes, and the L-BFGS default is still the capped eOn step.
  The ~#[repr(C)]~ structs of the C ABI keep their layout.
- rgsaddle builds on rgmin 0.6.0, pinned by full rev at ~=0.6.0~ here and in ~ci/consumer~.
  A host that passes rgmin types, such as the ~Method~ of ~BandConfig::with_method~, takes them from that rgmin, whose ~Method~ and ~LineSearch~ struct variants are non-exhaustive: a struct literal of one no longer compiles outside rgmin, and a constructor such as ~Method::fire(FireKind::V2)~, ~Method::lbfgs()~ or ~LineSearch::wolfe~ builds it.
  Every rgmin configuration in this crate is built from ~Default~ or a named constructor with ~with_*~ setters, and every ~Method~ and ~LineSearch~ variant through its constructor, so a field rgmin adds later does not break the build.
  FIRE 2.0 now follows Guénolé et al. (2020), Algorithm 2: Euler then mixing, a half step back after non-positive power with the force taken again at the corrected point, and no time-step decrease in the opening delay.
  A FIRE band step that backs up costs two evaluations of the interior images, and the recorded FIRE band, dimer, Lanczos and AutoNEB trajectories moved with the integrator.
  A capped FIRE translation that left the negative-curvature neighborhood restarts with half the time step, which FIRE 2.0's delay no longer does on its own.

** Added

- =ElasticMultipole= expands the atomic forces of a defect about one centre, the single-centre layout of Wikfeldt, Batista, Vila, and Jónsson, Phys. Chem. Chem. Phys. 15, 16542 (2013), doi:10.1039/C3CP52097H. =StrainResponse::barrier_in= subtracts half the saddle-minus-minimum quadrupole contracted with the strain gradient, when one is supplied. =Kelvin= is the isotropic Green's function. =DirectSolver= sums every pair and =FmmSolver= translates well-separated cells with a multipole-to-local step (Greengard and Rokhlin, J. - =PairNetwork= is the kinetic network of a mobile defect next to a fixed one, resolved by symmetry. States are orbits of the integer relative vector and orientation director under a caller-supplied group (=cubic_group= gives O_h), and hop classes are orbits of the unordered configuration pair and saddle director, so one saddle serves a hop and its reverse. =PairMove::orbit= closes a move list under the group. =PairNetwork::sites= asks a closure for each edge's rate, with its hop class, direction and source state, and returns =VolumeSite= rows for =kinetic_recombination_volume= and =PairExit= rows for the hops that leave the radius. Nothing in it names a material.
  =matched_capture_radius= closes a site chain at its cutoff against the field-free infinite crystal: an outer hop to =d= is captured with probability =R/d=, =R= matched to the multiplicity-weighted =P r= of the outer sites the caller lists. Capture probabilities are affine in =R=, so two solves give it in closed form. Each outer hop is an =OuterHop=, and the result is a =MatchedCapture=, the radius and the volume.

- =StressCheck= and =check_cauchy_stress= compare =PointSurface::cauchy_stress= with =finite_difference_cauchy_stress= of the same surface's energy entry by entry, each, by default, against 1e-6 plus 1% of the larger of its two values, and =SolidDimerConfig::with_stress_check= runs that comparison once before the first free-cell step. - =defect_pair_rates= searches a caller-supplied hop catalogue with the solid-state dimer, requires one negative mode, and checks that a push off both sides of the saddle returns to the named minima. A climbing-image band of =DefectPairConfig::images= images, 7 by default, is started from an IDPP path through that saddle. PAFI integrates =⟨(∇E · n)(1-ψ)⟩= (Swinburne and Marinica, Phys. Rev. Lett. 120, 135503 (2018), doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.120.135503). The rate is =κ ν exp(-ΔF/kT)=, with =ν= the reactant attempt frequency along the path tangent, =ω / 2π= with =ω² = τ · H τ / m=, and =κ= the Bennett–Chandler factor (Chandler, J. Chem. Phys. 68, 2959 (1978), doi:10.1063/1.436049). The Vineyard rate is stored beside it. =RateCatalogue= is =rgsaddle.defect_pair_rates= version 1. =howto/defect-pair-rates=.
  The climbing image is armed only after a plain relaxation of the band: with climbing armed from the first step, L-BFGS follows the highest image, so an IDPP image on a collision climbs the collision, and the band's guards reset each capped step without making that image the saddle. A diverging climb is repeated with FIRE, or with ODE12r when the caller was already on FIRE. The connectivity push is the largest atomic displacement. PAFI block errors use the integrated autocorrelation time, and a finite-temperature rate is stored only when the zero-temperature integral of the path force reproduces the static barrier.
  =DefectPairConfig::relax=, a =RelaxConfig=, drives every minimization: the reactant, the products and the pushes off each saddle run to =relax.force_tol= on the largest per-atom force, along the steepest-descent flow (=Method::Ode12r= under a 0.08 cap) by default, and reaching =relax.max_steps= is an error. =descend_to_floor= follows =config.relax= until the energy is at or below a floor or the largest per-atom force is below =relax.force_tol=, and fails on the step cap or a non-finite energy.
- =transmission_coefficient= assigns end states by the side of the plane (=EndState::Side=, the default) or by quenching into reference basins (=EndState::Quench=), optionally with the intensive permutation-invariant distance of Goswami and Jónsson (=PermutationDistance=), counts either Bennett–Chandler or first crossings (=Counting=), and shoots with a mass for every coordinate. The reversible work runs along any =PlanePath=: PAFI planes along a spline through a minimum energy path, the planes the optimizer accepted (=PlanePath::trace=), straight legs, or a chain of them (=reversible_work_along=). Between neighbouring planes BAR reports the two-state overlap and warns below =ReversibleWorkConfig::min_overlap=, MBAR combines all nodes (=with_mbar=), and =Transport::Gaussian= adds the Gaussian optimal-transport map within each plane, shrunk toward the rigid map by a James–Stein factor so that unresolved changes do not cost overlap. =basin_density= integrates the mean force with a refined not-a-knot spline over a window per side and uses Stein control variates from the Hessian at the minimum. =howto/oh-tst=.
