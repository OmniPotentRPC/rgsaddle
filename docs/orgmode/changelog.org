#+TITLE: Changelog
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

* Unreleased

- The band offers rgmin's quick-min variants. ~BandConfig::quickmin_options~ takes ~QuickMinOptions::rgmin~ (default), ~::eon~ or ~::per_atom~, ~BandSession::set_quickmin_options~ replaces it, and from C ~rgsaddle_band_set_quickmin_variant~ takes ~RGSADDLE_QUICKMIN_RGMIN~, ~_EON~ or ~_PER_ATOM~ on a band made with ~RGSADDLE_METHOD_QUICKMIN~. rgmin is pinned at d76539d.
- ~BandConfig::time_step~ (and ~BandSession::set_time_step~, ~rgsaddle_band_set_time_step~) sets the initial FIRE and quick-min time step, 1 by default; the eOn quick-min variant keeps it fixed. Band force calls to a largest component under 0.001 (7 interior images, CI from the start): EAM Al vacancy hop (601 atoms) FIRE 478, L-BFGS 233, quick-min rgmin 744, per-atom 569, eOn 779 at dt 0.1 and 625 at 0.25; Morse Pt heptamer process (336 atoms) FIRE 324, L-BFGS 79, quick-min rgmin 240 (163 at dt 0.1), per-atom 310, eOn 170 at dt 0.1 and 184 at 0.25. The eOn variant does not converge in 5000 steps at dt 0.5 or 1.
- The dimer measures its rotated dimer when the trial angle sits on its 1e-4 rad floor and the fit asks for an extrapolation weight past 8. With forward differences the fit divides a slope mismatch of ~dr/2 T[n,n,theta]~ by the trial angle; on a bent double well the extrapolated actions reported a curvature of -14.07 where the Hessian spectrum is [4.62, 10.05] (~validation/dimer_extrapolation.py~, ~tests/minmode_switch.rs~).
- Measured on eOn's Morse Pt heptamer saddle search (336 atoms, ~displacement.con~, 3 repeats, identical counts). With ~MinModeSession~ climbing by itself, the switched translation takes the dimer under FIRE from 63 steps and 138 force calls to 51 and 112, Lanczos under FIRE from 139 to 112 calls, the dimer under L-BFGS from 29 to 28 and Lanczos under L-BFGS from 30 to 29. Every run ends at -1462.00876 eV with curvature -1.010. eOn's own ~min_mode_method = xtsci~ search stays at 39 calls, because eOn asks rgsaddle only for the rotation and translates with its own optimizer.
- `Stencil::point` runs a minimiser, the dimer, Lanczos, ART, and eigen following. Dimer and Lanczos construct `MinModeSession`, and the golden hashes match. `Stencil::band` returns the `feat/band-methods-zoom` seam. `Chain::evaluate` writes fixed one-image blocks by index. The C ABI adds `rgsaddle_stencil_*` and thread-local `rgsaddle_last_error` without a minor bump. Python `Stencil.point` and `rgsaddle.ase.Point` call the C++ wrapper. Goswami, Gunde, and Jónsson, Front. Chem. (2026), doi:10.3389/fchem.2026.1807063, Eq. 9.
- `Surface` is the batched row trait. A type that implements it also implements `PointSurface` and `BandSurface`. `FromPoint` and `FromBand` lift an existing surface. Callers of the old traits are unchanged, and the golden hashes match.
- The band tangent, the spring coupling, and the climbing rules `reflect`, `perp`, and `ascend` go through one `Triad`. Goswami, Gunde, and Jónsson, Front. Chem. (2026), doi:10.3389/fchem.2026.1807063, Eq. 9, is the shared reflection. The golden position and energy hashes are unchanged.
- Golden hashes of raw little-endian f64 positions and energies, after a fixed step count, for neb, ci-neb, dneb, weighted-neb, dimer, Lanczos, and AutoNEB. Fixtures are the double well, Müller-Brown, and a three-atom harmonic chain. Threads 1, 2, 3, and 8 match.
- Breaking: the python cargo feature and the pyo3 dependency are removed.
- The Python package is a nanobind extension built with scikit-build-core and Corrosion. PyO3 and maturin are gone. The eight =IntEnum= controls keep the names and values of the previous module. =NumericalError=, =last_error= and =raise_status= map =rgsaddle_status_t=. =rgsaddle.ase.SurfaceAdapter= evaluates an ASE image list as C ABI request rows. Session types stay off the Python surface.
- Breaking: struct literals of ~Index1Config~, ~SparseHessianOpts~, and ~LowestEigenpairs~ no longer compile outside this crate, because each is ~#[non_exhaustive]~. ~Index1Config~ also gained ~sparse_pattern~. Migrate it with ~with_update~, ~with_mode~, ~with_trust_radius~, ~with_force_tol~, ~with_fd_dr~, and ~with_sparse_pattern~. ~Index1Config::default()~ is unchanged and the CPR seed stays off. Build ~SparseHessianOpts~ with ~Default~ and ~with_difference~, ~with_step~, ~with_memory_budget~, and ~with_skin~. Read ~LowestEigenpairs~ by its fields. The default step is ~1e-4~, the same width as ~fd_dr~. ~matvec~ panics on a length mismatch with the same message as ~apply_hessian~.
- ~sparse_hessian~ recovers a cutoff Hessian by a direct Curtis-Powell-Reid partition. ~distance2_colouring~ is greedy in smallest-last order. A block of colours is one batched surface call, sized by ~SparseHessianOpts::memory_budget~ rather than by the thread count, and the blocks are written back in colour order. Each geometry is its own chunk. ~from_positions~ builds the pattern with a linkcell cutoff walk plus ~search_skin~, which is ~max(2 * step, skin)~. ~lowest_eigenpairs~ is a thick-restart Lanczos with a residual stop, an iteration cap, and the residuals in the result. Its inner product is a fixed-order dot, serial below 65,536 elements. Powell and Toint (1979) cover the symmetric direct read and the substitution. Star colouring (Coleman and Moré 1984) stays a later option.
- AutoNEB stays in Rust on ~BandConfig::autoneb~. The C ABI stays at minor 16.
- AutoNEB records a trajectory only when ~record_samples~ is set. The default stores nothing and takes no sample lock. One stored call is one ~f64~ per degree of freedom.
- AutoNEB follows the ASE window and spring rescaling. The fill from two endpoints sets every segment to ~k * (n_simul + 1)~, and each insertion doubles the cut segment. The search stops after the climbing window unless ~full_band~ is set. On the double well that default used 1.42 times as many force calls as one fixed CI-NEB. Müller-Brown from B to C used 0.46 times as many, and the two-barrier path from A to B has mean ratio 0.95.
- AutoNEB reuses one band session while the window indices do not change, so the L-BFGS pairs survive the climb on that window. A new index set starts empty. Known endpoint energies are not evaluated again.
- AutoNEB image evaluation keeps one thread pool for the search. Each evaluation is a fixed set of contiguous blocks, stored by image index after every block has finished. One thread evaluates the batch in a single call. There is no SIMD path. The warm-step speedup is capped by ~n_simul~ free images.
- AutoNEB temporarily refuses a weighted or Onsager-Machlup spring. Zoom-NEB will recompute those springs when an image is inserted.
- AutoNEB grows a band from the supplied images. It inserts where the peak-weighted energy gap or the segment length is worst, relaxes that local window, and arms the climbing image on the last stage. The inserted image is the minimum-image midpoint.
- ~xts_minmode_estimate~ is the xtsci name of ~rgsaddle_minmode_estimate~. A null session still fails closed.
- ~xts_band_step~ is the xtsci name of ~rgsaddle_band_step~. The arguments and the status codes are the same.
- C ABI minor 16. ~RGSADDLE_METHOD_QUICKMIN~ runs quick-min on the band. ~rgsaddle_band_create_solid~ builds a solid-state band and ~rgsaddle_band_step_solid~ steps it: one ~rgsaddle_solid_surface_request_t~ carries each image's positions and cell in and its energy, gradient and Cauchy stress out. ~rgsaddle_band_cells~ and ~rgsaddle_band_solid_jacobian~ read the cells and the Jacobian back. ~BandConfig::quickmin~ selects the same step. A min-mode session returns null for that method code. FIRE stays the default.
- The band can carry the generalized solid-state cell block. The tangent acts on the atomic displacement plus the averaged, Jacobian-scaled strain, and the cell force is minus the cell volume times the Cauchy stress plus hydrostatic pressure, divided by the Jacobian. ~BandSurface::eval_cells~ supplies the stress. The Jacobian is fixed from the mean endpoint volume. ~SolidState~ is non-exhaustive: build it with ~SolidState::new~, ~with_pressure~ and ~with_weight~. ~validation/ssneb_jacobian.py~ derives the units, the strain-stress pairing and the rotation-free lower triangle.
- Minimum-mode translation follows the curvature. While the curvature along the mode is not negative the effective gradient is minus the parallel part and the perpendicular force is dropped. A negative curvature keeps the perpendicular force and reflects the parallel part. The dimer and Lanczos share that step. ~validation/minmode_switch.py~ derives both branches on a quadratic model, and the Lean ~MinMode~ contract states the switched force (~switchedForce~ and the ~switched_force_*~ theorems).
- C ABI minor 5. ~rgsaddle_band_restart~ restarts the band optimizer and keeps the endpoint energies and the last evaluation. ~rgsaddle_band_reset~ is the surface-change boundary and drops both. ~rgsaddle_band_evaluation~ returns the energies, gradients and projected force at the current band, or ~RGSADDLE_NO_EVALUATION~. The report's ~evaluations~ field takes the place of ~reserved~ and counts the surface rows of a step.
- C ABI minor 5, min-mode reuse. ~rgsaddle_minmode_set_position~ moves a session, with the host's gradient when it has one, ~rgsaddle_minmode_set_mode~ replaces the seed, and ~rgsaddle_minmode_estimate~ refreshes the lowest mode without a translation. ~rgsaddle_minmode_config_t~ gains ~rotation_angle_tol~ (radians; 0 turns the angle test off) and ~difference~ (~RGSADDLE_DIFFERENCE_FORWARD~ or ~_CENTRAL~), read only from configs whose version minor is 5 or newer; the Rust ~MinModeConfig~ carries the same fields.
- C ABI minor 14 adds the Sella minimum and saddle sessions, the reaction-path (IRC), SAMD, constraint and internal-coordinate PES sessions, the kappa dimer, Niggli reduction, and ~rgsaddle_band_set_force_gate~, ~rgsaddle_minmode_set_force_gate~, ~rgsaddle_band_set_highs~ and ~rgsaddle_minmode_set_highs~.
- C ABI minor 15: ~rgsaddle_sella_saddle_seed_mode~ seeds a Cartesian Sella saddle session's Hessian with a mode and its curvature, with no surface call.
- The band and min-mode sessions build on rgmin main (pinned by revision until an rgmin release carries it). They call ~Solver::forget_evaluation~ when their oracle changes at a fixed point, and the band's L-BFGS memory comes from its config.
- Arm eOn's L-BFGS resets on the band: a two-loop step that reaches the per-atom cap or faces away from the force drops the stored pairs and steps along the force, and an empty memory scales that step by 0.01.
- Climb the highest interior image only while it lies above both fixed endpoints, as eOn does. A monotonic band keeps the spring force on every image and reports no climbing image.
- Remove the mean Cartesian force from each multi-atom band image, as eOn does when every atom is free. ~BandConfig::remove_translation~ and the ~RGSADDLE_BAND_KEEP_TRANSLATION~ band flag keep the mean for hosts with fixed atoms.
- Retain main's band and minimum-mode solver contracts alongside the Sella, reaction-path, constraint, cell and dynamics interfaces.
- Require curvature measured at a moved stationary point before minimum-mode convergence.
- Update the restricted atomic step radius with the largest atom displacement.
- Preserve the complete cell when reading molecular frames.
- Expose typed C++ and Python controls and the official ABI 1.15 contract.
- Include the retained numerical trajectories, callback-count checks and symbolic, interval and Lean proofs.
