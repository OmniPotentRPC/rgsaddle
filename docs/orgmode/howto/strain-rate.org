#+title: Strain response of a hop
#+options: toc:nil num:nil

* What this is
=StrainResponse= is one migration event. It holds the zero-strain barrier, the elastic dipoles of the minimum and the saddle, an optional Vineyard prefactor and its strain slope, and the symmetry images of that hop. It does not store a strain grid. The strain at which a rate is needed is an argument of =barrier=.

* The dipole
Varvenne and Clouet, Phys. Rev. B 96, 224103 (2017), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.96.224103, define the interaction of a point defect with an applied strain as =-P : ε= (their Eq. (5)). On a fixed cell their Eq. (8) is =P = -V σ=. The paragraph after that equation removes a nonzero homogeneous stress of the perfect cell. =ElasticDipole::from_stress= is that correction:

: P = -V (σ_defect - σ_bulk)

and the result is symmetrized, because the defect has no net torque (their Eq. (1)). =from_forces= is the same tensor as the first moment of a set of Kanzaki forces with zero resultant and zero torque. Any potential and any cell can supply the stress or the forces. Nothing in the library is specific to copper.

The barrier of one hop is the difference of the two interaction energies:

: ΔE(ε) = ΔE0 - (P_saddle - P_minimum) : ε

Dederichs and Schroeder, Phys. Rev. B 17, 2524 (1978), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.17.2524, show that a homogeneous strain changes the jump rate through the saddle dipole relative to the equilibrium site. The activation volume =Ω = ∂ΔG/∂τ= is =activation_volume=.

* Higher moments
=ElasticMultipole::about= expands the atomic forces about one centre, the same single-centre idea as Wikfeldt, Batista, Vila, and Jónsson, Phys. Chem. Chem. Phys. 15, 16542 (2013), doi:10.1039/C3CP52097H. The dipole is the first moment. The quadrupole is =Q_ijk = Σ F_i X_j X_k= and the octupole is the next moment. =barrier_in= adds =-(1/2) (Q_saddle - Q_minimum)_ijk ∂_k ε_ij= when the caller supplies a strain gradient. A homogeneous =barrier= leaves that term out.

* Symmetry images
=with_point_group= rotates both dipoles by each supplied matrix, =P' = R P R^T=, and drops images that repeat a dipole. =cubic_rotations= is the 24 proper rotations of the cube. A caller that already knows the distinct hops can pass them to =with_variants= instead. =barrier= returns the image with the lowest barrier, and names it in =Estimate::variant=.

* Trust
=TrustPolicy= is the validity bound.

- =radius= is a cap on the Frobenius norm of =ε=.
- =quadratic= is a second-order error estimate, =quadratic * |ε|²=, compared with =error_tolerance= (0.01 eV by default, =LINEAR_FOLD_EV=).
- =curvature= is the unstable-mode curvature =λ0= and its strain slope. The line is trusted while =λ(ε) = λ0 + slope : ε= stays negative and at least as negative as =fraction * λ0=.
- =floor= is a barrier floor. A prediction at or below it needs a search, which is how a barrier that has collapsed is reported.

=Estimate::trust= is =Linear=, =Explicit= after a cached search, or =NeedsSearch= with the reason. =rate_hz= returns the Arrhenius rate only for =Linear= and =Explicit=.

* Refine
=refine= affine-strains a =SaddleSeed=, runs a fixed-cell =SolidDimer= with an index-1 check, and caches the explicit barrier. The surface is evaluated in Cartesian coordinates, so bind it to the strained cell first. =minimum_energy= is the reactant energy in that cell. A later =barrier= at the same strain returns =Trust::Explicit=.

* Serialization
=to_json= / =from_json= round-trip the event, including the policy, the images, and the explicit cache. The document name is =rgsaddle.strain_response=. An AKMC store can keep one document per event.

* The field of the other defects
The strain in =barrier= is the applied strain plus the strain produced by every other defect. =Kelvin= is the isotropic Green's function. =DirectSolver= sums it. =FmmSolver= walks an octree: neighboring leaves are direct, and a well-separated cell is translated into a local expansion, the multipole-to-local step of Greengard and Rokhlin, J. Comput. Phys. 73, 325 (1987), doi:10.1016/0021-9991(87)90140-9. =examples/elastic_field_bench.rs= times both on 100 to 100000 defects and writes =benchmarks/elastic_field_scaling.csv=.

* A sweep
=examples/akmc_strain.rs= places four copies of one hop along a shear that grows with the site coordinate, takes the trusted Arrhenius rate, and refines the sites past the radius.

* Copper check
=benchmarks/strain_response_cu.json= is the zero-strain dipole of the 109-atom Mishin copper =[100]= dumbbell and its =[110]= saddle, plus the quadratic error coefficient fitted at 0.5 percent shear. It is a validation record, not a rate table. At =ε_yx = 0.005= the line is inside 0.01 eV. At =ε_yx = 0.02= the quadratic estimate is past 0.01 eV, so =barrier= returns =NeedsSearch=. The same barrier is returned for =ε_zx = 0.02= from the rotated image of the hop.

* It does not save calls at equal accuracy
=benchmarks/strain_response_head_to_head.csv= is a release run of the ignored test =strain_response_head_to_head=. The test system is the same 109-atom Mishin EAM1 dumbbell. The numbers below are that file. Nothing here is estimated.

The eOn values 0.47381310, 0.24354609, 0.14829202, 0.06786217 and 0.00779374 eV, and the variable-cell values 0.34700694, 0.16255572, 0.08805194 and 0.02792458 eV, are the xy simple shear of that dumbbell, not a vacancy hop. On TheochemUI/eOn branch =feat/solid-state-dimer= at e96b0881 the test is "copper dumbbell saddle follows the shear-aligned switch" in =client/unit_tests/SolidStateDimerTest.cpp=, and =docs/source/user_guide/solid_state.md= ("Copper interstitial") describes it. The cell map is =h = L * [[1, 0, 0], [γ, 1, 0], [0, 0, 1]]=, so =x' = x + γ y=, on a 3×3×3 cell with one <100> dumbbell (109 atoms). Fixed barriers are at 0, 2, 3, 4 and 5%. Variable-cell enthalpy barriers are at 0, 2, 3 and 4%. At 5% variable that test's reactant is a minimum and the index-1 point is not the shear-aligned <110> switch, so it is not a barrier. The full digits are that branch's =benchmarks/solid_dimer_cu.csv=.

The explicit fixed-cell search here, minus those eOn barriers, is +0.0001031420501363, +0.0000167051625795, -0.0000251251088343, -0.0000192768926581 and -0.0000325910417018 eV at 0, 2, 3, 4 and 5%. The variable-cell enthalpy minus the eOn enthalpy is +0.0000194914556459, +0.0004562000656279, -0.0004392792773729 and -0.0004913819460626 eV at 0, 2, 3 and 4%. At 5% variable this search is also not the switch (=explicit_is_barrier= 0, reactant lowest eigenvalue +0.0188089858488028). The enthalpy it did stop on is 0.9154464168817640 eV, which is a different index-1 point from eOn's and is not the barrier either.

=StrainResponse::barrier= with the quadratic bound fitted at 0.5% xy shear (coefficient 68.1263486858729692, tolerance 0.01 eV) does not reproduce those barriers once the line leaves that neighborhood, and the refine hook does not repair it. The one-time build is 2696 force calls: 2 for the bulk stress, 1355 for the zero-strain saddle, 1339 for the 0.5% calibration search. A force multipole about the relaxed atoms was refused, because the residual forces are not torque-free, so the build is the stress dipole only. Both rates use one prefactor. The relative rate error is =exp(-(E_method - E_explicit) / kT) - 1= with =k= = =BOLTZMANN_EV_PER_K=. The prefactor cancels. The response object carries 1 Hz only so a rate can be formed.

On the 25 fixed-cell points (xy, xz, uniaxial x, hydrostatic, at 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5%, with 0% stored once) the explicit searches cost 32152 calls. The method costs 102225, the build plus every refine attempt. Eleven points that =NeedsSearch= were still =Running= after 2500 steps, so the reported barrier is the dipole line. Six refine calls did stop on an index-1 cache entry. Hydrostatic 2% matches the explicit barrier to +0.0000032056108807 eV and spends 5255 calls against an explicit search of 871. Hydrostatic 5% matches to -0.0001842612500127 eV and spends 2821 against 2162. The other four do not match: xy 5% and xz 5% both return 1.0629406385345419 eV and 1.0629406385357925 eV against explicit barriers of 0.0077611463732410 eV and 0.0077611571679199 eV; xz 4% is high by 0.0225712541990788 eV; hydrostatic 4% is high by 3.7771389058863747 eV.

Where the line is trusted and no refine runs, the largest absolute barrier error outside the calibration point is 0.0067987321227992 eV, on xz at 1%. The relative rate error there is 0.3008094186029679 at 300 K and 0.1405303234035331 at 600 K. A barrier inside the 0.01 eV fold is not the same rate.

The 200-event batch is the field in =examples/akmc_strain.rs=: four sites, a saddle dipole with =P_xy = 8=, Kelvin modulus 40 and Poisson ratio 0.3, spacing 6, plus =Strain::shear_yx(0.01)=, then symmetrized. All four stay on the line, so the queries add 0 calls. With the build, the method costs 2696. The four sites are two strains. One explicit search at each costs 1485 and 1290 calls, sum 2775. Fifty events at each site, each event searching again, cost 277500. The barrier errors are -0.0069910033479984 eV and -0.0072082400189121 eV. The 300 K relative rate errors are 0.3105201028103695 and 0.3215789197167316. The 600 K ones are 0.1447794996462723 and 0.1495994605586468. Repeating a trusted query skips the search. It does not return the explicit rate. One search at each of the two strains costs 2775 calls, against 2696 for the build.

Variable-cell rows are the two shears, including 0%. Uniaxial and hydrostatic are imposed strains, so only the fixed cell is the ensemble the dipole line claims. The 13 variable searches cost 18072 calls. The method does not answer those enthalpies.
