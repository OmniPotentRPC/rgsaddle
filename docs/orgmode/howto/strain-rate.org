#+title: Strain response of a hop
#+options: toc:nil num:nil

* What this is
=StrainResponse= is one migration event. It holds the zero-strain barrier, the elastic dipoles of the minimum and the saddle, an optional Vineyard prefactor and its strain slope, and the symmetry images of that hop. It does not store a strain grid. The strain at which a rate is needed is an argument of =barrier=.

* The dipole
Varvenne and Clouet, Phys. Rev. B 96, 224103 (2017), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.96.224103, define the interaction of a point defect with an applied strain as =-P : ε= (their Eq. (5)). On a fixed cell their Eq. (8) is =P = -V σ=. The paragraph after that equation removes a nonzero homogeneous stress of the perfect cell. =ElasticDipole::from_stress= is that correction:

: P = -V (σ_defect - σ_bulk)

and the result is symmetrized, because the defect has no net torque (their Eq. (1)). =from_forces= is the same tensor as the first moment of a set of Kanzaki forces with zero resultant and zero torque. Any potential and any cell can supply the stress or the forces. Nothing in the library is specific to copper.

The barrier of one hop is the difference of the two interaction energies:

: ΔE(ε) = ΔE0 - (P_saddle - P_minimum) : ε

Dederichs and Schroeder, Phys. Rev. B 17, 2524 (1978), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.17.2524, show that a homogeneous strain changes the jump rate through the saddle dipole relative to the equilibrium site. The activation volume =Ω = ∂ΔG/∂τ= is =activation_volume=.

* Higher moments
=ElasticMultipole::about= expands the atomic forces about one centre, the same single-centre idea as Wikfeldt, Batista, Vila, and Jónsson, Phys. Chem. Chem. Phys. 15, 16542 (2013), doi:10.1039/C3CP52097H. The dipole is the first moment. The quadrupole is =Q_ijk = Σ F_i X_j X_k= and the octupole is the next moment. =barrier_in= adds =-(1/2) (Q_saddle - Q_minimum)_ijk ∂_k ε_ij= when the caller supplies a strain gradient. A homogeneous =barrier= leaves that term out.

* Symmetry images
=with_point_group= rotates both dipoles by each supplied matrix, =P' = R P R^T=, and drops images that repeat a dipole. =cubic_rotations= is the 24 proper rotations of the cube. A caller that already knows the distinct hops can pass them to =with_variants= instead. =barrier= returns the image with the lowest barrier, and names it in =Estimate::variant=.

* Trust
=TrustPolicy= is the validity bound.

- =radius= is a cap on the Frobenius norm of =ε=.
- =quadratic= is a second-order error estimate, =quadratic * |ε|²=, compared with =error_tolerance= (0.01 eV by default, =LINEAR_FOLD_EV=).
- =curvature= is the unstable-mode curvature =λ0= and its strain slope. The line is trusted while =λ(ε) = λ0 + slope : ε= stays negative and at least as negative as =fraction * λ0=.
- =floor= is a barrier floor. A prediction at or below it needs a search, which is how a barrier that has collapsed is reported.

=Estimate::trust= is =Linear=, =Explicit= after a cached search, or =NeedsSearch= with the reason. =rate_hz= returns the Arrhenius rate only for =Linear= and =Explicit=.

* Refine
=refine= affine-strains a =SaddleSeed= and runs a fixed-cell =SolidDimer=. The per-atom cap is 0.02 Å. The 0.2 Å minimum-escape step is the wrong length here: the predicted saddle can sit a tenth of an angstrom away, on the side where the lowest curvature is still positive, and one step of 0.2 Å leaves that basin and stops on a different index-1 saddle. The force gate is 2e-3. The 1e-3 default of =MinModeConfig= is past the point where a 0.02 Å step still fits =|F|/|λ|=, so once the curvature is negative FIRE throws the force back up and repeats the cycle. The energy in that cycle is under 1 meV. 2e-3 is the gate the climb from the minimum already uses. Shrinking the cap to =|F|/|λ|= on each step does not end the cycle. A result is cached only when it is index 1 and no atom has moved farther than the supplied hop and farther than 0.4 Å. If the refined point is more than 0.08 Å RMS from the prediction, one downhill quench at a force gate of 0.01 must land within 0.05 eV of =minimum_energy=. A point inside 0.08 Å RMS, with the curvature already negative, skips that quench: the wrong index-1 points seen on this hop sit several tenths of an angstrom away. Otherwise =refine= returns an error and does not cache the point. =refined_saddle= is that geometry. A JSON round trip keeps the barrier and drops the coordinates. The surface is evaluated in Cartesian coordinates, so bind it to the strained cell first. =minimum_energy= is the reactant energy in that cell. A later =barrier= at the same strain returns =Trust::Explicit=.

* Serialization
=to_json= / =from_json= round-trip the event, including the policy, the images, and the explicit cache. The document name is =rgsaddle.strain_response=. An AKMC store can keep one document per event.

* The field of the other defects
The strain in =barrier= is the applied strain plus the strain produced by every other defect. =Kelvin= is the isotropic Green's function. =DirectSolver= sums it. =FmmSolver= walks an octree: neighboring leaves are direct, and a well-separated cell is translated into a local expansion, the multipole-to-local step of Greengard and Rokhlin, J. Comput. Phys. 73, 325 (1987), doi:10.1016/0021-9991(87)90140-9. =examples/elastic_field_bench.rs= times both on 100 to 100000 defects and writes =benchmarks/elastic_field_scaling.csv=.

* A sweep
=examples/akmc_strain.rs= places four copies of one hop along a shear that grows with the site coordinate, takes the trusted Arrhenius rate, and refines the sites past the radius.

* Copper check
=benchmarks/strain_response_cu.json= is the zero-strain dipole of the 109-atom Mishin copper =[100]= dumbbell and its =[110]= saddle, plus the quadratic error coefficient fitted at 0.5 percent shear. It is a validation record, not a rate table. At =ε_yx = 0.005= the line is inside 0.01 eV. At =ε_yx = 0.02= the quadratic estimate is past 0.01 eV, so =barrier= returns =NeedsSearch=. The same barrier is returned for =ε_zx = 0.02= from the rotated image of the hop.

=StrainResponse::barrier= with the quadratic bound fitted at 0.5% xy shear (coefficient 68.1263486858729692, tolerance 0.01 eV) leaves the explicit barrier once the line leaves that neighborhood. =refine= is what runs there. The one-time build is 2696 force calls: 2 for the bulk stress, 1355 for the zero-strain saddle, 1339 for the 0.5% calibration search. A force multipole about the relaxed atoms was refused, because the residual forces are not torque-free, so the build is the stress dipole only. Both rates use one prefactor. The relative rate error is =exp(-(E_method - E_explicit) / kT) - 1= with =k= = =BOLTZMANN_EV_PER_K=. The prefactor cancels. The response object carries 1 Hz only so a rate can be formed.
