~IrcSession~ traces the intrinsic reaction coordinate (IRC) on either side of a supplied saddle.
The saddle and the mode it leaves along can come from the climbing image aligned with the Hessian eigenmode (Goswami, Gunde, and Jonsson, Front. Chem. 14 (2026), doi:10.3389/fchem.2026.1807063), and two-dimensional root-mean-square deviation (RMSD) projections compare a branch with a band (Goswami, MethodsX 16, 103851 (2026), doi:10.1016/j.mex.2026.103851).
Each call to ~step~ performs one outer IRC move, of one of three kinds.

- ~IrcKind::SellaSphere~, the default, takes the energy minimum on the mass-weighted sphere of radius ~dx~ about the last point, the first about the saddle. Mueller and Brown constrain the step to that sphere (Theor. Chim. Acta 53, 75 (1979), doi:10.1007/BF00547608). Sella's ~IRCTrustRegion~ takes the same step (Hermes, Sargsyan, Najm, and Zador, J. Chem. Theory Comput. 18, 6974 (2022), doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.2c00395). Its path error is first order in ~dx~.
- ~IrcKind::Gs2~ is the step of Gonzalez and Schlegel, J. Chem. Phys. 90, 2154 (1989), doi:10.1063/1.456010. Its pivot sits ~dx/2~ down the mass-weighted gradient from the last point, or ~dx/2~ along the launch mode from the saddle for the first step. The next point is the energy minimum on the sphere of radius ~dx/2~ about that pivot. Both points lie on one circular arc tangent to the gradient at each of them. The path error is therefore second order in ~dx~. Radial projection maps a model minimum between the last point and the pivot back onto the last point. Until the model is positive definite and the arc exceeds eight radii, a model minimum inside the sphere therefore carries the step on along its line to the far side. The report's ~arc~ adds the mass-weighted chord of each step.
- ~IrcKind::Morokuma~ selects the predictor-corrector path.

~tests/irc_mueller_brown.rs~ fits both orders on the Mueller-Brown surface.

~new~ accepts a molecular Cartesian packing and atom masses. ~new_euclidean~ accepts an arbitrary nonzero dimension with unit coordinate weights. ~from_surface~ estimates the launch mode through Hessian actions as the lowest eigenvector of the mass-weighted Hessian. A host can instead pass a measured mode to ~new~.

~set_direction~ restores the saddle and reverses the launch. ~reset~ clears the approximation at a surface change. A sphere step rejects an energy increase above its specified numerical threshold and reduces the radius.
Each later step without a rejection doubles the radius back up to the requested one.
~set_direction~ starts each branch at the requested radius.

Step until the report sets ~at_minimum~.
A sphere-step report sets it once the step is past the kick and the largest force is at or below ~force_tol~.
One Hessian action at that point must also give positive curvature along the launch mode.
That action is the only extra surface call, and it is made only once the force is at or below ~force_tol~.
The sphere radius does not enter, so a branch whose radius was halved still stops on its force.
A point beside the saddle, where the curvature along the mode is still negative, does not stop.
Through the C interface, ~rgsaddle_irc_step~ writes ~RGSADDLE_STATUS_CONVERGED~ to the report's ~status~ on that step.

~tests/irc_termination.rs~ checks that stop on both branches from two saddles that eOn hands over.
These are the 13-atom Lennard-Jones cluster (LJ13) and the Morse Pt adatom.
The default run checks the LJ13 reverse branch at ~force_tol~ 0.005 and eight Pt adatom branches.
The full LJ13 set, both branches at 0.05, 0.01 and 0.005, is ignored in a debug build:

Select ~L2Norm~ or ~LinfNorm~ for a generic vector packing. ~MaxForceOnAtom~ groups Cartesian triples. The C interface exposes construction, stepping, position access, direction changes, reset and destruction; ~tests/c/irc_abi.c~ exercises both directions of both sphere kinds and the Morokuma path. ~RGSADDLE_IRC_SELLA_SPHERE~ needs ABI minor 34. A config whose version minor is 33 or older runs the Sella sphere for every kind but ~RGSADDLE_IRC_MOROKUMA~, and from minor 34 an unknown kind makes create return NULL.
