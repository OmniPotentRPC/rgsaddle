#+title: Step a point stencil
#+options: toc:nil num:nil

:cite:`goswamiEnhancedClimbingImage2026`

=Stencil::point= builds a minimiser, a dimer, Lanczos, ART, or eigen following. The defaults match =MinModeConfig=: forward difference, =dr = 1e-3=, rotation tolerance =1e-4=, 20 rotations, Krylov dimension 12, FIRE, maximum move 0.2. Dimer and Lanczos are a =MinModeSession=. A negative curvature reflects the parallel force. A curvature that is not negative ascends along the mode and drops the perpendicular force. Henkelman and Jónsson (1999), doi:10.1063/1.480097. Goswami, Gunde, and Jónsson, Front. Chem. (2026), doi:10.3389/fchem.2026.1807063, Eq. 9.

#+begin_src rust
let mut search = Stencil::point(x0)
    .preset(Preset::Dimer)
    .mode(seed)
    .build()?;
let report = search.step(&surface)?;
#+end_src

ART pushes along the seed until the curvature falls under =art_threshold= (default 0), then climbs with Lanczos. Malek and Mousseau (2000), doi:10.1103/PhysRevE.62.7723. Eigen following builds a central-difference Hessian, calls =exact_eigh=, and makes the largest-magnitude component of the lowest mode positive. =build= rejects =Climb::Reflect= on a minimiser, a window on a point, a zero thread count, and ART without a mode solver.

=Stencil::band= runs NEB, doubly nudged elastic band, CI-NEB, energy-weighted springs, zoom-NEB, AutoNEB, OCI-NEB, IDPP, and S-IDPP. Zoom ends stay fixed unless the builder asks for perpendicular ends. The chain, the mesh (remesh, insert, window), and the point controller share this stencil. IDPP follows Smidstrup, Pedersen, Stokbro, and Jónsson, J. Chem. Phys. 140, 214106 (2014), doi:10.1063/1.4878664. S-IDPP follows Schmerwitz, Ásgeirsson, and Jónsson, arXiv:2310.04531. Neither evaluates the caller surface.

From C, =rgsaddle_stencil_config_new= takes the preset discriminant. Unknown keys return =RGSADDLE_INVALID_PARAMETER=. =rgsaddle_last_error= is thread-local. =rgsaddle_stencil_run= loops over =rgsaddle_stencil_step=. The report opens with =struct_size=. ABI minor 17 adds the band keys, starting at 11, and the trailing report fields. The older band and min-mode entries stay step-only.

From Python, =rgsaddle.Stencil.point(x, preset="dimer")= takes a C-contiguous float64 vector. =step(surface)= releases the thread, and the surface callback re-attaches. =rgsaddle.ase.Point(atoms, preset="dimer").run(fmax, steps)= is an ASE =Optimizer= whose =step= is one stencil step. A second thread that enters the same handle raises =RuntimeError=.

=Chain::evaluate= writes each image from a block of one. Threads 1, 2, 3, and 8 produce the same bytes. A point preset is one row, so its thread count does not change the bytes either.
