#+TITLE: Move a search from ASE or eOn
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

The Rust sessions are the source of the mechanics. The Python classes in ~rgsaddle.ase~ follow the call shape of ASE's nudged elastic band, minimum-mode atoms, and Sella's ~run(fmax, steps)~. Where the numbers differ, the difference is the one the tests lock in.

* From ASE's NEB
~rgsaddle.ase.NEB(images, k=0.1, climb=False)~ takes the same list of ~Atoms~ with calculators. ~interpolate(method='linear')~ places the interior images on the straight line between the ends, and ~mic=True~ uses ASE's minimum-image displacement of those ends, and the Rust minimum image follows the same periodic axes, so a slab or wire with a non-periodic axis is not folded across its vacuum. ~interpolate(method='idpp')~ replaces that line with ~idpp_path~, the image-dependent pair potential. ASE's IDPP stops an MDMin run; this path is L-BFGS, so a bond that both keep near its target length is not the same coordinate. ~apply_constraint=None~ raises ~RuntimeError~ when a constraint other than ~FixAtoms~ would move an atom. ~get_positions~ and ~get_forces~ are the interior images only, and ~ase.optimize.FIRE~ drives them through ~__ase_optimizable__~. ~get_potential_energy~ is the highest image energy.

The spring constant defaults to 0.1, ASE's value. A ~BandSession~ still defaults to 5, eOn's value. Pass the number you mean.

~method='improvedtangent'~ is the default, and an omitted method warns. ~method='aseneb'~ builds that same improved-tangent force. ASE's ~aseneb~ is a different, unpublished tangent and spring, so the two forces are not the same. ~eb~ is the plain elastic band, not ASE's full spring from the elastic-band paper. ~dneb~ adds the doubly nudged correction. ~spline~ and ~string~ raise ~UnsupportedFeature~: those names are the preconditioned band of Makri, Ortner, and Kermode, and the simplified string method is ~SimplifiedString~. The exponential metric is ~BandConfig.exp_precon~ on the Rust session, so ~precon~ on this class raises ~UnsupportedFeature~. ~parallel=True~, and a ~world~ whose ~size~ is not 1, raise ~UnsupportedFeature~ because the images are evaluated on the calling thread. A list of equal spring constants is accepted as that one value. A list of unequal springs raises ~UnsupportedFeature~.

~climb=True~ climbs on the call, with no arming delay. It does not climb when a higher endpoint slopes down into the band. ASE climbs the highest image anyway. That image then feels the reflected force and walks up a slope that has no saddle between it and the endpoint. ~tests/python/test_ase_dropin.py~ and ~tests/api_input.rs~ lock the refusal. A ~BandSession~ still waits until the band force drops before it arms the climb; the one-shot projection does not, because the caller already asked for it.

~remove_rotation_and_translation=True~ removes the mean Cartesian force. It does not rotate an image onto its neighbour. A Kabsch rotation would change the geometry the calculator evaluates, and the unit tangent already fixes the path direction.

~FixAtoms~ on the images is read into the band's fixed-atom mask, as ~rgsaddle_band_set_fixed_atoms~ takes it from C. The fixed atoms get zero force rows, keep the mean force even with ~remove_rotation_and_translation=True~, and stay where the first image has them through ~interpolate()~. Every image must fix the same atoms. Other constraints act through ~get_forces()~ as before.

~DyNEB~ is the dynamic band of Lindgren, Kastlunger, and Peterson, J. Chem. Theory Comput. 15, 5787 (2019), doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.9b00622. An image whose force is already under ~fmax~ is left in place, and its force row is zero, except the highest interior image. ~scale_fmax~ loosens that test with the distance from the highest image. ~NEB~ itself keeps ~dynamic_relaxation=False~. Passing ~dynamic_relaxation=True~ to ~NEB~ warns and opts in. ~scale_fmax~ without that flag raises ~ValueError~.

~AutoNEB~ reads ~prefix000.traj~ and ~prefix001.traj~ and grows the band with ~autoneb_run~. The constructor arguments match ~ase.mep.AutoNEB~. The steps are the band's FIRE solver. ~maxstep~ is read from the optimizer class signature when that parameter has a default. ~smooth_curve=True~, a non-FIRE optimizer, and ~method~ ~spline~ or ~string~ raise ~UnsupportedFeature~. The final images are written back to ~prefixNNN.traj~. Per-iteration trajectory files are not written. The initial fill matches ASE when ~n_simul~ is 1 or 3.

* From ASE's dimer and from Sella
~rgsaddle.ase.MinModeAtoms(atoms, control=None, eigenmodes=None, *, mode=..., method='lbfgs')~ estimates a dimer or Lanczos mode and returns the effective force. A ~DimerControl~ carries ASE's parameter names, one copy per instance. ~eigenmode_method~, ~dimer_separation~, ~max_num_rot~, ~f_rot_min~, and ~maximum_translation~ are the kind, the finite-difference length, the rotation cap, the rotation residual, and the per-atom step cap. ~order~ other than 1, a displacement mask, extrapolated forces, and ~use_central_forces=False~ raise ~UnsupportedFeature~. A knob this step does not read raises ~UnsupportedFeature~ when it is not the default, so a changed ~trial_angle~ or ~f_rot_max~ is not a silent no-op. Above the well the perpendicular force is dropped. Below it the force along the mode is reflected. ~MinModeTranslate(dimeratoms, logfile='-', trajectory=None)~ is an ASE ~Optimizer~ subclass whose first argument is the ~MinModeAtoms~: each step is one Rust minimum-mode translation, and a ~DimerControl~ also requires a negative curvature. The translation is the core's L-BFGS step unless ~MinModeAtoms~ is built with ~method='fire'~; it is not ASE's Newton line search. An ASE optimizer can drive ~MinModeAtoms~ directly as well.

~rgsaddle.ase.Sella(atoms, order=1)~ is the saddle optimizer. ~run(fmax, steps)~ is ASE's loop. One step is the partitioned rational-function trust step, not a dimer translation. Masses come from ~atoms.get_masses~.

* From eOn
The band tangents, springs, projections, and the climbing-image arm follow eOn's branch structure. ~CiConfig::after(0.5)~ is eOn's ~ci_after~. ~after_rel~ is ~ci_after_rel~. ~always~ climbs from the first evaluation. The minimum-mode dimer is eOn's improved dimer: ~rotation_angle_tol~ defaults to five degrees (~converged_angle~), and a rotation stops when the trial or the fitted angle falls under it, on ~rotation_tol~, or at ~max_rotations~. It does not infer the classic torque window; ~rotation_angle_tol~ 0 restores that window at the stock residual, and ~set_torque_window(Some(true))~ adds it to the improved dimer. Lanczos stops on the window at two vectors. eOn's Lanczos stops on the relative change of its lowest Ritz value (~lanczos_tolerance~), which ~set_lanczos_rel_tol~ sets here without the window.

eOn's client reads a config file and a potential plugin. Here the host passes positions and a surface. There is no system name, species table, or potential key in the session. A solid-state band takes one cell per image and the Cauchy stress through ~BandSurface::eval_cells~.

Live Onsager-Machlup displacements are the atomic force over ~2k~. A solid-state band has a cell block in the same vector, so that spring is refused at construction rather than applied as zeros.
