#+title: Defect-pair hop rates
#+options: toc:nil num:nil

* What is computed
=defect_pair_rates= takes any =PointSurface=, a periodic cell, one relaxed defect geometry, and a catalogue of hops. A hop is a product geometry with a name. The library does not know what a vacancy or a dumbbell is. The caller names the fixed point, the atoms whose centroid is the mobile defect, and a shell label.

For each hop the search

1. minimizes the reactant and the product,
2. climbs from the reactant side of the hop with =SolidDimer=, on a frozen cell or on the joint chart,
3. keeps the saddle only when =IndexCheck= reports exactly one negative projected eigenvalue,
4. pushes off both sides of the unstable mode by a largest-atomic displacement of =push=, minimizes, and accepts the hop when the two clouds of atoms match the reactant and the product (Hausdorff distance, so a permutation of identical atoms still matches),
5. builds an image-dependent-pair-potential path through that saddle, relaxes it with climbing off, then climbs. The climb uses the caller's band method. If that climb runs away, it is repeated with FIRE, or with ODE12r when the caller was already on FIRE,
6. integrates the projected average force along that path and shoots the Bennett–Chandler factor from the free-energy maximum.

AutoNEB, zoom, and image freezing are not used. The band has a fixed number of images.

A hop is =toward= when the mobile centroid moves closer to the fixed defect by more than the tolerance, =away= when it moves farther, and =tangential= otherwise. =classify_separation= is that test.

* The rate
The finite-temperature rate is

: k(T) = κ ν exp(-ΔF / kT)

=ΔF= is the potential of mean force from the reactant hyperplane to the dividing surface, the maximum of the PAFI profile. =ν= is the reactant attempt frequency along the path tangent only,

: ν = (1 / 2π) sqrt( (τ · H τ) / m )

with =τ= the unit tangent at the reactant and =H= the Cartesian Hessian. =κ= is the Bennett–Chandler transmission coefficient. In the harmonic limit =ΔF= holds the transverse vibrational entropy, and this expression agrees with Vineyard's rate when =κ = 1=. Vineyard, J. Phys. Chem. Solids 3, 121 (1957), doi:10.1016/0022-3697(57)90059-8.

The comparison rate is the full Vineyard prefactor,

: k_h(T) = ν_h exp(-ΔE0 / kT),    ν_h = (∏ ν_min) / (∏ ν_sad),

translations removed when the Hessian is the whole cell, the unstable eigenvalue left out of the saddle product. =ΔE0= is the potential difference, or the enthalpy difference when an external stress is set. The harmonic free-energy barrier implied by the same split is

: ΔF_h = ΔE0 - kT ln(ν_h / ν).

=ΔF - ΔF_h= is the anharmonic excess. At equal mass, energy in eV and length in Å, =angular_scale_rad_s= turns =sqrt(λ / m)= into rad/s and the rates are in Hz. A caller in another unit system sets =boltzmann=, =mass= and =angular_scale=.

* PAFI
Swinburne and Marinica, Phys. Rev. Lett. 120, 135503 (2018), doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.120.135503, give the free-energy gradient along a path without a collective variable. The estimator implemented here is the one averaged by LAMMPS =fix pafi= (Swinburne's implementation of that paper). With =n = dX/ds= and =ψ = (x - X) · dn/ds=,

: dF/ds = ⟨ (∇E · n) (1 - ψ) ⟩.

The average is over the hyperplane =(x - X) · n = 0=. Sampling is overdamped Langevin (Brownian) dynamics in that plane: the parallel piece of the drift and of the noise is removed, the plane is restored after every step, and the center of mass can be held. The integral is the trapezoid rule. The standard error comes from block means whose block is at least twice the integrated autocorrelation time of the recorded series, propagated through the trapezoid weights. When =target_error= is set, planes are resampled and inverse-variance combined until the barrier error is at or below that target or =max_rounds= is spent. The dividing surface is the plane of maximum =ΔF=. Sitting on the path with =ψ = 0= is the zero-temperature limit of the same integral. A finite-temperature rate is stored only when that limit reproduces =ΔE0= within =zero_t_tol= and the band force is under tolerance.

On a straight path =ψ= is zero and the estimator is the mean force. A one-dimensional barrier =(1 - x²)²= integrates to 1. A two-dimensional well =(1 - x²)² + ½ k(x) y²= with =k = 1.5 + 0.5 x= has the exact classical shift =(kT / 2) ln(k(0) / k(-1))=. =tests/pafi_analytic.rs= checks both.

* Bennett–Chandler
Bennett, in Algorithms for Chemical Computations, ACS Symposium Series 46, 63 (1977), doi:10.1021/bk-1977-0046.ch004, and Chandler, J. Chem. Phys. 68, 2959 (1978), doi:10.1063/1.436049, write the recrossing factor as the plateau of the flux correlation. Configurations are the PAFI samples on the dividing surface. Velocities are Maxwell–Boltzmann. The trajectories are inertial Langevin (BAOAB); zero friction is Newtonian.

: κ(t) = ⟨ v_τ(0) θ(s(t)) ⟩ / ⟨ v_τ(0) θ(v_τ(0)) ⟩

=s(t) = (x(t) - x_surface) · τ= and =θ(s)= is 1 on the product side of the surface. The numerator keeps the sign of the initial velocity. The reported =κ= is =κ(t)= at the requested plateau record, with a block standard error. A rate is formed only when =κ= lies in =[0, 1]=.

On the quartic =V = (x² - 1)²= the barrier curvature gives =ω_b = 2= at unit mass. Newtonian trajectories that start at the top with =v > 0= are still on the product side a short time later, so =κ = 1=. With friction =γ = 2=, Kramers, Physica 7, 284 (1940), doi:10.1016/S0031-8914(40)90098-2, gives

: κ = sqrt(1 + α²) - α,    α = γ / (2 ω_b) = 1/2,

which is =sqrt(5)/2 - 1/2 ≈ 0.618=. =tests/bennett_kramers.rs= compares the plateau of =κ(t)= with that value.

* The catalogue
=RateCatalogue::to_json= writes =rgsaddle.defect_pair_rates= version 1. =from_json= reads it back. Each entry is one exit channel. The key is =(shell, direction, hop, temperature, cell_repeats)=. =rate_Hz= is =k(T)= in the caller's frequency unit (Hz when the angular scale is =angular_scale_rad_s=).

amsel is the absorbing-Markov selector for superbasin exit distributions. It does not parse this file itself. A loader groups entries that share a shell, a cell and a temperature, and passes =rate_Hz= to the FPTA or MRM kernel as the first-order exit rate of that channel. =hop= and =direction= are the channel name. =dE0=, =dF=, =dF_error=, =dF_harmonic=, =nu=, =vineyard=, =kappa=, the saddle Cauchy stress and the elastic dipole =P = -V (σ - σ_bulk)= are stored beside the rate so a later run can see what entered =k(T)=.

* Assumptions
The dividing surface is a hyperplane normal to the path, not a variationally optimized surface. The harmonic prefactor uses a caller-chosen block of atoms when the whole-cell Hessian is too large; modes below =10⁻⁴= in the surface's curvature unit are left out of the product. Overdamped sampling assumes the constrained ensemble has been reached after =thermalize= steps. The Bennett–Chandler plateau is whatever record the caller names; a negative =κ= means that record is not a transmission coefficient and no rate is stored. The cell of a frozen search is the cell bound into the surface.

* Copper
The Mishin EAM1 numbers are a validation, not part of the library. The potential is the analytic form already in =tests/mishin_cu.rs= (Mishin, Mehl, Papaconstantopoulos, Voter, and Kress, Phys. Rev. B 63, 224106 (2001), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.63.224106, Table I). The question is the one posed by Mwungeli, Liu, and Demkowicz, Phys. Rev. Materials 9, 023601 (2025), doi:10.1103/PhysRevMaterials.9.023601: whether the vacancy / self-interstitial recombination volume in Cu falls from about 290 Ω at 300 K to about 160 Ω at 500 K and above because the hop rates toward, away from, and tangential to the vacancy are anharmonic. Crossref records the paper as published 11 February 2025, Phys. Rev. Materials 9, 023601. Their Table 1 gives the zero-pressure lattice parameters 3.631, 3.637, 3.643, 3.649 and 3.655 Å at 300, 400, 500, 600 and 700 K, and cells of 10, 11, 12, 13 and 16 cubic repeats (4000, 5324, 6912, 8788 and 16384 atoms).

Climbing L-BFGS follows the highest image. On the copper band that image was a collision left by the initial path, and the step ran up the wall; the NEB guards drop such a step and do not make it the saddle. The band used here relaxes with FIRE and climbing off, then climbs. A path is kept only when the force is at most 0.01 eV/Å and the zero-temperature integral of the path force reproduces ΔE0.

The path integral is the line integral of ∇E along the polyline through the images. A cubic spline between those images cut the corner off a curved saddle, and the rotation integral came out 14 meV low. On the polyline, 122 rated translation hops on the 8×8×8 cell have |ΔE0 − zero-temperature integral| at most 0.0053 eV. The free dumbbell's rotation was not found by the seeds used here, so its ΔF is still not stored.
