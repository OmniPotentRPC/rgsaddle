#+TITLE: Follow a minimum mode
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

~MinModeSession~ estimates a low-curvature direction by dimer rotation or Lanczos, then advances on the min-mode force. L-BFGS takes ~Accept::Step~ and rgmin's NEB guards: the reflected force is not a potential gradient (Goswami, Gunde, and Jónsson, Front. Chem. (2026), doi:10.3389/fchem.2026.1807063, Eq. 9), an empty memory scales the step by 0.01, and a two-loop step that reaches the per-atom cap or faces away from the force drops its pairs. Lanczos stops when the relative change in the lowest Ritz value falls under ~rotation_tol~, or when the residual does, and a repeat visit whose Rayleigh quotient still agrees with the retained curvature returns after one product (Olsen, Kroes, Henkelman, Arnaldsson, and Jónsson, J. Chem. Phys. 121, 9776 (2004), doi:10.1063/1.1814098; eOn ~quit_early~). While the curvature along that mode is not negative the step is one full per-atom ~max_move~ along the mode (the perpendicular force is dropped). Once the curvature is negative the perpendicular force stays and the parallel part is reflected, and the L-BFGS pairs and the FIRE velocity survive a mode turn inside 45 degrees. A turn past that cone drops the memory. Lanczos uses the same translation. At the stock ~rotation_tol~ of 1e-4 the dimer also stops after one rotation once the rotational force is under 1; a tighter residual keeps iterating. ~FiniteDifference~ selects forward or central Hessian actions. A surface can supply analytic actions and excluded directions through ~PointSurface~.

The reported force norm must satisfy the selected ~ForceGate~, and the estimated curvature must be negative. If a translation reaches a stationary point, the session measures curvature at that accepted position before reporting convergence. This directional test does not certify the full Hessian index.

~set_kappa~ selects the basin constraint. ~set_highs~ selects the optional feasible-step control. Call ~reset~ at a surface change and use ~set_force_gate~ to select the physical stopping norm. Hosts that require exactly one imaginary mode must perform their full index check.

** Keep one session across a host search

A host that climbs with its own optimizer keeps one session for the whole search. At each new centre it calls ~set_position~ (~rgsaddle_minmode_set_position~) with the gradient it already has, so the session does not evaluate the centre, then ~estimate_mode~ (~rgsaddle_minmode_estimate~). The previous mode seeds the next rotation, so an unchanged mode costs one evaluation. ~set_mode~ (~rgsaddle_minmode_set_mode~) replaces the seed. All three need ABI minor 5.

Lanczos through ~rgsaddle_minmode_estimate~ advances one Hessian action and keeps the Krylov basis until ~set_position~, ~set_mode~, or ~reset~. Repeat the call until the host's own test on the mode passes. The basis grows to the free dimension rather than stopping at ~krylov_dim~. After k actions the returned mode lies in the k + 1 Lanczos vectors: the last is the residual direction the k-th action already produced. In the Lanczos basis an eigenvector's components are the Lanczos polynomials at its eigenvalue (Parlett, The Symmetric Eigenvalue Problem, SIAM (1998), doi:10.1137/1.9781611971163, Ch. 7), and only the known k rows of the tridiagonal enter. The session evaluates them at an estimate of the lowest eigenvalue: the next Ritz value predicted with the last Lanczos diagonal, extrapolated with the two previous Ritz values by Aitken's delta-squared (Aitken, Proc. R. Soc. Edinburgh 46, 289 (1927), doi:10.1017/S0370164600022070) when those fall with shrinking steps. At the Ritz value the same formula is the plain Ritz vector. No surface call is added, and the reported curvature is still the Ritz value of the k vectors, an upper bound on the lowest eigenvalue. Sella 0.1.1 (Hermes, Sargsyan, Najm, and Zádor, J. Chem. Theory Comput. 15, 6536 (2019), doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.9b00869) expands by the same residual at this stage, where it has no Hessian yet to precondition with, and tests the Ritz vector of the vectors it has applied the Hessian to. On ~lowest-eigenvector/lj38~ that iteration and the plain Ritz vector both take about 16.8 actions per start. ~MinModeSession::estimate_mode~ and ~step~ still finish the residual test before a translation (Olsen, Kroes, Henkelman, Arnaldsson, and Jónsson, J. Chem. Phys. 121, 9776 (2004), doi:10.1063/1.1814098). A dimer estimate is the full rotation.

These ~MinModeConfig~ fields set the cost of an estimate and of the translation:

| Field | Effect | Default |
|---+---+---|
| ~difference~ | ~Forward~: one gradient per Hessian action, error O(dr). ~Central~: two, error O(dr^2) | ~Forward~ |
| ~rotation_angle_tol~ | The dimer stops rotating when an iteration's optimal angle falls under this many radians; 0 turns the test off | five degrees |
| ~rotation_tol~ | Rotational-force residual. At 1e-4 and any looser value the dimer also stops after one rotation once that force is under 1. A tighter value keeps iterating. Lanczos uses it as the Ritz residual and, with no host relative tolerance, as the relative change of the lowest Ritz value | 1e-4 |
| ~max_rotations~ | Rotation cap for one dimer estimate | 20 |
| ~max_move~ | Largest per-atom translation. A curvature that is not negative takes one step of this length along the mode. The L-BFGS pairs and the FIRE velocity are kept while the mode turns by at most 45 degrees, and dropped past that cone | 0.2 |

From C, ~difference~ and ~rotation_angle_tol~ sit in ~rgsaddle_minmode_config_t~ and are read only when the config's ~version.minor~ is 5 or more. A zeroed C struct leaves ~rotation_angle_tol~ at 0, so the angle test stays off. The C++ helper ~minmode_config~ does that, and sets ~rotation_tol~ to 1e-3, which is loose enough for the torque window. The Python point stencil does not take ~rotation_angle_tol~; it uses the Rust default of five degrees. ~rotation_tol~ on that stencil defaults to 1e-4.
