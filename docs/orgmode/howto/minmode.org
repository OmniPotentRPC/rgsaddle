~MinModeSession~ estimates a low-curvature direction by dimer rotation or Lanczos, then advances on the min-mode force. L-BFGS takes ~Accept::Step~ and rgmin's NEB guards: the reflected force is not a potential gradient (Goswami, Gunde, and Jónsson, Front. Chem. (2026), doi:10.3389/fchem.2026.1807063, Eq. 9), an empty memory scales the step by 0.01, and a two-loop step that reaches the per-atom cap or faces away from the force drops its pairs. Lanczos stops when the relative change in the lowest Ritz value falls under ~rotation_tol~, or when the residual does, and a repeat visit whose Rayleigh quotient still agrees with the retained curvature returns after one product (Olsen, Kroes, Henkelman, Arnaldsson, and Jónsson, J. Chem. Phys. 121, 9776 (2004), doi:10.1063/1.1809574; eOn ~quit_early~). While the curvature along that mode is not negative the step is one full per-atom ~max_move~ along the mode (the perpendicular force is dropped). Once the curvature is negative the perpendicular force stays and the parallel part is reflected, and the L-BFGS pairs and the FIRE velocity survive a mode turn inside 45 degrees. A turn past that cone drops the memory and zeros the FIRE velocity. FIRE's timestep stays 1. From rest the move is a quarter of the reflected force, because a zero power halves the step before the Euler update. When the per-atom cap shortens that step and the arrival is no longer negative, the move is replaced from the last negative point by 0.01 times the reflected force, the empty-memory scale of the L-BFGS guard. A capped step that stays negative is kept. L-BFGS is unchanged, and no C field was added. Lanczos uses the same translation. At the stock ~rotation_tol~ of 1e-4 the dimer also stops after one rotation once the rotational force is under 1; a tighter residual keeps iterating. ~FiniteDifference~ selects forward or central Hessian actions. A surface can supply analytic actions and excluded directions through ~PointSurface~.

The reported force norm must satisfy the selected ~ForceGate~, and the estimated curvature must be negative. If a translation reaches a stationary point, the session measures curvature at that accepted position before reporting convergence. This directional test does not certify the full Hessian index.

~set_kappa~ selects the basin constraint. ~set_highs~ selects the optional feasible-step control. Call ~reset~ at a surface change and use ~set_force_gate~ to select the physical stopping norm. Hosts that require exactly one imaginary mode must perform their full index check.

** Keep one session across a host search

A host that climbs with its own optimizer keeps one session for the whole search. At each new centre it calls ~set_position~ (~rgsaddle_minmode_set_position~) with the gradient it already has, so the session does not evaluate the centre, then ~estimate_mode~ (~rgsaddle_minmode_estimate~). The previous mode seeds the next rotation. Dimer rotations also retain their Polak--Ribiere force and direction across a translation; a repeated estimate at exactly one centre restarts that rotational history. A failed surface evaluation leaves the retained rotation history unchanged, so a recoverable callback error does not silently turn the next estimate into a fresh rotation. ~set_mode~ (~rgsaddle_minmode_set_mode~) replaces the seed and clears both rotational history and the carried curvature. ~reset~ clears them at a surface-epoch boundary. All three need ABI minor 5. They added no C field.

Lanczos through ~rgsaddle_minmode_estimate~ advances one Hessian action and keeps the Krylov basis until ~set_position~, ~set_mode~, or ~reset~. Repeat the call until the host's own test on the mode passes. The basis grows to the free dimension rather than stopping at ~krylov_dim~. After k actions the returned mode lies in the k + 1 Lanczos vectors: the last is the residual direction the k-th action already produced. In the Lanczos basis an eigenvector's components are the Lanczos polynomials at its eigenvalue (Parlett, The Symmetric Eigenvalue Problem, SIAM (1998), doi:10.1137/1.9781611971163, Ch. 7), and only the known k rows of the tridiagonal enter. The session evaluates them at an estimate of the lowest eigenvalue: the next Ritz value predicted with the last Lanczos diagonal, extrapolated with the two previous Ritz values by Aitken's delta-squared (Aitken, Proc. R. Soc. Edinburgh 46, 289 (1927), doi:10.1017/S0370164600022070) when those fall with shrinking steps. At the Ritz value the same formula is the plain Ritz vector. No surface call is added, and the reported curvature is still the Ritz value of the k vectors, an upper bound on the lowest eigenvalue. Sella 0.1.1 (Hermes, Sargsyan, Najm, and Zádor, J. Chem. Theory Comput. 15, 6536 (2019), doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.9b00869) expands by the same residual at this stage, where it has no Hessian yet to precondition with, and tests the Ritz vector of the vectors it has applied the Hessian to. On ~lowest-eigenvector/lj38~ that iteration and the plain Ritz vector both take about 16.8 actions per start. ~MinModeSession::estimate_mode~ and ~step~ still finish the residual test before a translation (Olsen, Kroes, Henkelman, Arnaldsson, and Jónsson, J. Chem. Phys. 121, 9776 (2004), doi:10.1063/1.1809574). A dimer estimate is the full rotation.

These ~MinModeConfig~ fields set the cost of an estimate and of the translation:

| Field | Effect | Default |
|---+---+---|
| ~difference~ | ~Forward~: one gradient per Hessian action, error O(dr). ~Central~: two, error O(dr^2) | ~Forward~ |
| ~rotation_angle_tol~ | The dimer stops rotating when an iteration's optimal angle falls under this many radians; 0 turns the test off | five degrees |
| ~rotation_tol~ | Rotational-force residual. At 1e-4 and any looser value the dimer also stops after one rotation once that force is under 1. A tighter value keeps iterating. Lanczos uses it as the Ritz residual and, with no host relative tolerance, as the relative change of the lowest Ritz value | 1e-4 |
| ~max_rotations~ | Rotation cap for one dimer estimate | 20 |
| ~max_move~ | Largest per-atom translation. A curvature that is not negative takes one step of this length along the mode. The L-BFGS pairs and the FIRE velocity are kept while the mode turns by at most 45 degrees, and dropped past that cone | 0.2 |
| ~seed_curvature~ | The first empty L-BFGS step uses ~H0 = 1/abs(λ)~, and the first FIRE displacement uses that scale, when the step fits in ~max_move~. Later steps are the stepper's own. ~set_seed_curvature~ sets the same switch on a live session | off |

From C, ~difference~ and ~rotation_angle_tol~ sit in ~rgsaddle_minmode_config_t~ and are read only when the config's ~version.minor~ is 5 or more. A zeroed C struct leaves ~rotation_angle_tol~ at 0, so the angle test stays off. The C++ helper ~minmode_config~ does that, and sets ~rotation_tol~ to 1e-3, which is loose enough for the torque window. The Python point stencil does not take ~rotation_angle_tol~; it uses the Rust default of five degrees. ~rotation_tol~ on that stencil defaults to 1e-4.

~set_torque_window~ sets the torque window apart from ~rotation_tol~.
From C ABI minor 32 ~rgsaddle_minmode_set_torque_window~ does the same.
~Some(false)~ keeps a loose residual without the window.
eOn's improved dimer does that with its ~torque_min~ of 0.1.
~Some(true)~ adds the window to a tighter residual.
~None~, the default, infers the window from ~rotation_tol~.

** Seed the first translation from the mode curvature

~MinModeConfig::seed_curvature~ is off unless a host sets it, and ~MinModeSession::set_seed_curvature~ does the same on a session that already exists. The C configuration and the point stencil leave it off, so those searches step as before. While the curvature ~λ~ along the mode is negative, no basin constraint is set, and the displacement ~F/|λ|~ fits in ~max_move~, one translation uses that scale. L-BFGS does it when its memory is still empty, and stores the secant of that step. FIRE does it on its first move. Every later step is the stepper's own update, still capped by ~max_move~. A step that would only hit the cap is left to the stepper: that capped move does not depend on ~|λ|~. ~reset~ arms the seed again.

eOn 3.6.0 is the source of the L-BFGS half. ~MinModeObjectiveFunction::knownCurvature~ returns ~-λ~ once the mode curvature is negative, and ~LBFGS::getStep~ sets ~H0 = 1/known~ on the optimizer's first iteration (https://github.com/TheochemUI/eOn/blob/v3.6.0/client/MinModeSaddleSearch.cpp and ~client/LBFGS.cpp~). The record doi:10.5281/zenodo.18529496 is TheochemUI/eOn v2.9.0.1. In that release, and at tag v2.17.0, the opening scale is still a finite difference along the force, so that record is not this seed. After the first step the scale is ~s·y / y·y~ (Liu and Nocedal, Math. Program. 45, 503 (1989), doi:10.1007/BF01589116). The reflected force those steps follow is Equation 3 of Goswami, Gunde, and Jónsson, Front. Chem. 14, 1807063 (2026), doi:10.3389/fchem.2026.1807063. FIRE's later steps are its own integrator (Bitzek, Koskinen, Gähler, Moseler, and Gumbsch, Phys. Rev. Lett. 97, 170201 (2006), doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.97.170201).

** A stalled FIRE step on a stiff mode

FIRE 2.0 keeps timestep 1 through its initial delay (Guénolé, Nöhring, Vaid, Houllé, Xie, Prakash, and Bitzek, Comput. Mater. Sci. 175, 109584 (2020), doi:10.1016/j.commatsci.2020.109584). The force it integrates is the reflected force, Equation 3 of Goswami, Gunde, and Jónsson, Front. Chem. 14, 1807063 (2026), doi:10.3389/fchem.2026.1807063. On the 255-atom vacancy the curvature along the mode is near -40 to -100, so the harmonic length ~|F|/|λ|~ is far under the atom cap of 0.2. The cap shortens the step, the next update adds the force again at timestep 1, and the search limit-cycles. ~set_fire_curvature~, on by default, waits until neither the force nor the energy has dropped for 2000 steps and ~|λ|~ is past 25, then takes one step of length ~|F|/slope~ along the reflected force, at most 0.05, provided the energy does not rise by more than 1e-3. A shorter trial is tried when that step would. The probe then stays in place while the curvature remains that steep, so timestep 1 cannot rebuild the cycle. A search that is still descending is unchanged. ~set_fire_curvature(false)~ is the previous integrator. L-BFGS does not read the switch. The switch added no C field.

~set_cg_translate~ replaces the translator with a Polak-Ribiere step perpendicular to the mode and a Newton step along it (Kästner and Sherwood, J. Chem. Phys. 128, 014106 (2008), doi:10.1063/1.2815812; Baker, J. Comput. Chem. 7, 385 (1986), doi:10.1002/jcc.540070402). ~set_lbfgs_project~ drops the component of each stored L-BFGS pair along the mode after a turn inside the 45 degree cone. Both are off.

** Buffers
A dimer estimate keeps one set of buffers for all its rotations: the
rotational force, its conjugate direction, and the trial and rotated
dimers.
Lanczos keeps its Krylov vectors, residual, coefficients and the tables
of its Ritz solve in the session, and moves each Hessian action into the
Krylov block it hands back.
A finite-difference action shifts the point in a buffer of its call and
forms the difference in the gradient the surface returned.
Outside the surface, a longer rotation or a larger Krylov space
allocates nothing more, and every estimate and step is bitwise what it
was.
The dimer is the reflection of Goswami, Gunde and Jónsson,
Front. Chem. 14 (2026), doi:10.3389/fchem.2026.1807063, Eq. 9,
and Goswami, ACS Phys. Chem. Au 6, 633 (2026), doi:10.1021/acsphyschemau.6c00038
(Eqs. 2.14-2.16 of the preprint, arXiv:2603.10992), on the rotation
of Henkelman and Jónsson, J. Chem. Phys. 111, 7010 (1999),
doi:10.1063/1.480097.
The Lanczos estimate is that of Olsen, Kroes, Henkelman, Arnaldsson
and Jónsson, J. Chem. Phys. 121, 9776 (2004), doi:10.1063/1.1809574.

the midpoint of its adatom hop, seeded with the hop direction, in the
