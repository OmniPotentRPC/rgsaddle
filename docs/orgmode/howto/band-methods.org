#+TITLE: Band methods on one stencil
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

=Stencil= is one session. =Stencil::band= runs plain NEB, CI-NEB, energy-weighted springs, AutoNEB, and zoom-NEB. =Stencil::point= runs the dimer, Lanczos, ART, eigen following, and a minimiser. ART climbs with Lanczos after the push.

Goswami, Gunde, and Jónsson, Front. Chem. 14, 1807063 (2026), doi:10.3389/fchem.2026.1807063, is the climbing-image and dimer account this crate follows. Zoom-NEB follows eOn 3.6.0, doi:10.5281/zenodo.23166734. Energy-weighted springs follow Ásgeirsson et al., J. Chem. Theory Comput. 17, 4929 (2021), doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.1c00462. AutoNEB follows Kolsbjerg, Groves, and Hammer, J. Chem. Phys. 145, 094107 (2016), doi:10.1063/1.4961868.

#+begin_export rst
:cite:`goswamiEnhancedClimbingImage2026,eon2901,kolsbjergAutomatedNudgedElastic2016`
#+end_export

There is no SIMD path, no BLAS, LAPACK, FFT, or GEMM call, and no MPI. Image blocks are a fixed partition. The raw bits do not depend on the thread count or on which block finishes first.

* Build
=+Stencil::band(positions)+ starts CI-NEB. =+point(x0)+ starts a dimer. =build= returns =Invalid= for a pair the stencil cannot run:

| Pair | Message |
|------+---------|
| =k_min > k_max= | energy-weighted springs need k_min at most k_max |
| one atom, or a length that is not a multiple of three, on IDPP or S-IDPP | coordinates must come in groups of three, or the path needs at least two atoms |
| a band preset on =Stencil::point= | the preset name, then "is a band preset" |
| a point preset on =Stencil::band= | the preset name, then "is a point preset" |

* Springs
Uniform =k = 5= eV/Å² is the default. Energy-weighted constants are recomputed from the energies of the assembly in progress. For segment =i=, =E_i= is the higher of the two image energies, =E_max= is the highest energy on the band, and =E_ref= is the higher endpoint. Above =E_ref=,

: alpha = clamp((E_max - E_i) / (E_max - E_ref), 0, 1)
: k = (1 - alpha) * k_max + alpha * k_min

Otherwise =k = k_min=. When =E_max - E_ref < 1e-10=, including =E_max = E_ref=, every constant is =k_min=. That is the continuous extension of a zero denominator. The climbing image does not use its constants. Defaults are =k_min = 0.972= and =k_max = 9.72= eV/Å², which are ORCA's 0.01 and 0.1 Eh/Bohr². Onsager-Machlup stays on =BandConfig= and is refused by the stencil until the host passes the displacement list.

* Force
Reports use the maximum absolute component (=ForceGate::LinfNorm=). The field =force_gate= is the string "max-abs-component". With a climbing image and no =image_scale=, only the climbing image must meet =ci_tol=, and any other moving image above =band_slack * ci_tol= (default 10) fails the step. =image_scale = Some(s)= also requires the other interiors under =s * ci_tol=. No climbing image means every interior is under =ci_tol=.

* Threads
=threads = 1= is one surface call. =threads > 1= evaluates one contiguous block per call. The first =n % ranks= ranks own one extra image. Writes land by image index after every block has finished. There is no cross-thread sum. AutoNEB keeps its own pool, so the stencil thread count is the pool size it is given. A Python callable with =threads > 1= raises =ValueError=. The GIL is released around the Rust step and taken again inside the callback.

A release timing on this 8-core host (1 thread per core, no SMT) used 18 images, so the fixed-end ceiling is 16 interiors and the free-end ceiling is 18. Each row burned about 1.1 ms and then evaluated the LJ13 potential. Five interleaved repeats of six steps gave, for CI-NEB, median 0.126 s, 0.066 s, 0.035 s, and 0.034 s at 1, 2, 4, and 8 threads (efficiency 1.00, 0.96, 0.90, 0.47). Zoom with fixed ends was the same shape once the pack had fired. Zoom with free ends was slower at one thread (0.137 s) and reached efficiency 0.52 at 8 threads. The serial fraction on that surface stayed near 0.04 until 8 threads, where it rose to about 0.15. Müller-Brown with no added burn was 0.1 ms at one thread and slower with more threads, so the limiter on a cheap surface is pool dispatch. On the slow surface it is the serial tangent, spring, and stepper assembly after the blocks join. The position hashes matched at every thread count.

* IDPP
IDPP follows Smidstrup, Pedersen, Stokbro, and Jónsson, J. Chem. Phys. 140, 214106 (2014), doi:10.1063/1.4878664. The ideal distance of each atom pair on image =l= is the linear interpolation of the reactant and product distances. The objective is the sum of squared distance errors weighted by the inverse fourth power of the current distance. =preset = idpp= relaxes that objective with the band optimizer and the improved tangent. Endpoints stay fixed. The caller surface is not evaluated. A row must be a multiple of three, with at least two atoms, and the band needs at least three images. The energy and the Cartesian force match the ASE =IDPP= calculator, including both triangles of the pair matrix and the factor of one half. Translation is left in the force.

* S-IDPP
S-IDPP follows Schmerwitz, Ásgeirsson, and Jónsson, arXiv:2310.04531. The linear interiors are discarded. Images are added from the reactant and the product, one side at a time, and the long central gap uses a spring =k * d_id / gap=. The tangent normalises each chord before the weighted average. After every requested image is present, equal springs refine the path. The caller surface is not evaluated. ASE has no S-IDPP calculator. The base spring is the band =k=. Each inner placement stops at a maximum force component of 0.01, an RMS of 0.005, or 40 steps.

* OCI-NEB
OCI-NEB follows Goswami, Gunde, and Jónsson, Front. Chem. 14, 1807063 (2026), doi:10.3389/fchem.2026.1807063. CI-NEB runs until the climbing index has stayed put for five steps and the climbing force is under 0.31 times the initial peak force. The dimer then follows the lowest mode, starting along the band tangent. A lower climbing force updates the threshold to =F_new * (0.5 + 0.4 * F_new / F_CI)= and redistributes the images on equal arc length. A poor alignment sets the next threshold to =F_0 * 0.31 * (0.5 + 0.5 * alpha)=, with =alpha = |d · tau|=. The alignment tolerance is 0.85. Ásgeirsson et al., J. Chem. Theory Comput. 17, 4929 (2021), doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.1c00462, is the static one-shot handover. ASE has no OCI-NEB. The dimer translates the reflected force with a Newton step `|F| / |λ|`, capped by the band maximum move, and keeps the lowest-force point. Positive curvature stops the walk. A walk that never lowers the force restores the saved image. On the double well that finishes in 95 force calls against 371 for CI-NEB. On Müller-Brown TS1 it finishes in 653 force calls against 1045. Both climbing images are within 1e-3 of the analytic saddle, and the RMS of the Cartesian gradient is under the force tolerance.

* What is not implemented
The tangent-overlap P-RFO hand-off is not wired. The old =rgsaddle_band_*= symbols are unchanged. =SpringKind::EnergyWeighted { k_min, k_max }= is a Rust enum variant. The historical C band spring codes stay 0, 1, and 2. The stencil spring key is 0 for a uniform spring and 1 for the energy-weighted law.
