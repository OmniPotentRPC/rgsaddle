* Band and minimum-mode
The band and the minimum-mode search assemble a force and ask =rgmin::Solver= for one step. Hosts interleave trust regions, drift budgets, acquisition, or a hybrid minimum-mode following (MMF) between steps. =run= is a loop over =step=. The shared reflection, the point presets, and the chain are in @@rst::doc:`one stencil <explanation/one-stencil>`@@.

* Index-1 Newton
=Index1Session= searches an index-1 saddle on a Cartesian energy Hessian. The displacement is Baker's restricted-step partitioned RFO (J. Comput. Chem. 7, 385 (1986), doi:10.1002/jcc.540070402): a 2 by 2 rational-function problem maximizes the lowest mode, a second rational-function problem minimizes the complement, and the scaling of Besalú and Bofill (Theor. Chem. Acc. 100, 265 (1998), doi:10.1007/s002140050387) is solved so the Cartesian step lies inside the trust sphere. The Powell update or the update of Bofill (J. Comput. Chem. 15, 1 (1994), doi:10.1002/jcc.540150102) corrects the Hessian from the gradient difference. The trust radius shrinks when that model predicts the energy change poorly and grows when a step on the sphere agrees with it.

=nichols_step= is the separate level shift of i-PI, from Nichols, Taylor, Schmidt and Simons, J. Chem. Phys. 92, 340 (1990), doi:10.1063/1.458435, and Simons and Nichols, Int. J. Quantum Chem. 38, 263 (1990), doi:10.1002/qua.560382427. A host that already holds a spectrum can call it. =rgsaddle_prfo_step= is the restricted-step partition. =rgsaddle_hessian_powell= and =rgsaddle_hessian_bofill= update the Hessian.

* eOn branch structure
Tangents, springs, projections, and the climbing-image force follow eOn's =NEBTangent= / =NEBSpringForce= / =NEBForceProjection= with the same branches. The climbing image arms when the convergence force falls under =factor * baseline= or under an absolute trigger.

* Periodicity
Positions stay unwrapped Cartesian. =Cell= wraps per-atom differences for the band mechanics through fractional coordinates (rows are lattice vectors; a zero row is a non-periodic axis). Neighbor lists, when a surface wants them, are vesin's job.

* The solid-state reference cell
The solid-state band is checked against an answer computed without a band. The case is the Bain path from bcc to fcc of LJ (epsilon 1 eV, sigma 1 A), switched to zero over [2.0, 2.2] A. Both atoms of the body-centred tetragonal cell diag(a, a, c) sit on inversion centres, so the atomic force is zero at any cell. The relaxed energy at fixed c/a is then a one-dimensional minimum over the volume. =validation/bain_lj_reference.py= scans it on a 2.5e-4 grid. bcc and fcc are minima, and the saddle sits at c/a 1.09331985, 0.0590356373 eV/atom above bcc. Over the six cell strains that saddle has one negative eigenvalue.

The saddle is not stable against atomic motion. The phonon at k = (1/2, 0, 1/2) of the conventional cell has the eigenvalue -51.5 eV/A^2 there, and it shuffles alternate planes. The two-atom cell carries no wave vector but k = 0, so in that cell the scan's saddle is the band's saddle. A supercell does carry the shuffle. A 5x5x4 supercell (200 atoms) has k = (2/5, 0, 1/2), with eigenvalue -38.4 eV/A^2. Its band, run with the settings of the two-atom test and a tolerance of 1e-3, shows the instability:

| iteration | largest force component | climbing image                      |
|-----------+-------------------------+-------------------------------------|
|       150 | 4.6e-3                  | c/a 1.0933, the scan saddle         |
|       200 | 3.19                    | cells shear, a and b part           |
|       947 | 8.9e-4 (converged)      | c/a 1.0523, 0.0266 eV/atom above bcc |

At convergence the cells carry a shear of 0.233 A, a and b differ by 0.065 A, and the atoms sit up to 0.031 (fractional) off their sites. Rounding seeds the shuffle. The band then follows it to a lower path, as an NEB should, and the scan is no reference for that cell. =tests/c/solid_bain_lj.c= therefore checks the two-atom cell. There the climbing image reaches c/a 1.09331984 and 0.0590356373 eV/atom. Run with arguments =5 5 4 7 1e-3 5000 0.1 0.5=, it reproduces the supercell run and fails its tetragonal-path check by design.

* Evaluations per step
A warm band step costs =n_images - 2= image evaluations. The endpoint energies are evaluated once. The session keeps its last evaluation, keyed on the interior positions, so a step that starts where the previous one ended is assembled from it: a host resync through =set_positions= (rows equal up to the minimum image keep the session's own copy), an optimizer =restart=, or a solver that dropped its own cache costs nothing. =BandReport.surface_rows= counts what the surface evaluated. The climbing image is chosen on the energies of the evaluation it climbs on, so the step that arms it already climbs.

* Steppers
FIRE 2.0 (Guénolé et al., Comput. Mater. Sci. 175, 109584 (2020), doi:10.1016/j.commatsci.2020.109584) is the default. eOn's FIRE is the original of Bitzek et al. (2006). The projected NEB force is not the gradient of the summed image energy the oracle reports, so L-BFGS runs under rgmin's =Accept::Step=: the two-loop direction, capped per atom, with one evaluation and no energy test.

* Minimum mode
The dimer rotation is the modified Newton step of Heyden, Bell, and Keil, J. Chem. Phys. 123, 224101 (2005), doi:10.1063/1.2104507, and Kästner and Sherwood, J. Chem. Phys. 128, 014106 (2008), doi:10.1063/1.2815812. One trial gradient fixes the Fourier model of the curvature in the rotation plane. The optimal angle then has a closed form. The gradient at the rotated dimer is a combination of the two measured ones, exact on a quadratic. Each rotation therefore costs one evaluation. Rotation planes are Polak-Ribiere conjugate. The force and direction of a finished rotation are kept for the next centre. A repeated estimate at the same centre, =set_mode=, and =reset= start that sequence again. A failed surface callback leaves the retained pair unchanged. Lanczos stops when the Ritz residual =beta_j |s_j|= meets =rotation_tol=. Both use the forward difference against the centre gradient by default; =validation/fd_curvature.py= gives its error, =dr |T| / 2 + 2 eps / dr=, and the =dr= that minimizes it for a given gradient noise. =FiniteDifference::Central= trades one more gradient per action for an =O(dr^2)= error.

The translation steps on the inverted force, whose value (the true energy) is not its potential, as the band's pseudo-energy is not the potential of the projected force. Both sessions accept only methods that step on the force alone (FIRE, L-BFGS under =Accept::Step=, Barzilai-Borwein, quick-min and ODE12r) and refuse line-searched ones. Between steps the solver's cached force, inverted along the previous mode, is dropped (rgmin =forget_evaluation=) and the centre answers the re-read without a surface call. A turn inside =MODE_RESET_ANGLE= (45 degrees, the Householder cone of Goswami, Gunde, and Jónsson, Front. Chem. 14 (2026), doi:10.3389/fchem.2026.1807063; =validation/mode_reset.py=) keeps the L-BFGS pairs and the FIRE velocity. Past that cone the memory is dropped and the FIRE velocity returns to zero. FIRE keeps timestep 1 (Bitzek, Koskinen, Gähler, Moseler, and Gumbsch, Phys. Rev. Lett. 97, 170201 (2006), doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.97.170201). From rest a zero power halves the step, so the move is =F / 4=. When the per-atom cap shortens that step and the arrival is no longer negative, the move is replaced from the last negative point by =0.01 F=. A capped step that stays negative is kept. A non-negative curvature takes one full per-atom =max_move= along the mode. At the stock rotational residual the dimer stops on eOn's torque window after one rotation; =rotation_angle_tol= defaults to five degrees and zero disables that angle test.
