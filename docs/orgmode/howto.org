#+title: How-to
#+options: toc:nil num:nil

* Step a band from C
Enable =capi=. The header is =rgsaddle.h=. ABI major 1. Every wire struct opens with =rgsaddle_version_t=. The host:

1. creates a session
2. calls =step= until the report says converged, or until host policy stops
3. reads =rgsaddle_band_evaluation= for the energies and forces at the new band instead of evaluating it again
4. calls =rgsaddle_band_restart= to restart the optimizer on the same surface, =rgsaddle_band_reset= at a surface-epoch boundary
5. frees

=rgsaddle_band_set_positions= with the band the host read back (even wrapped into its cell) costs no evaluation; only moved rows retire the cached values. =rgsaddle_report_t.evaluations= counts the image evaluations of each step.

The band and min-mode entries have no run-to-completion call. =rgsaddle_stencil_run= and =rgsaddle_connect= run to completion. Units are the caller's. Positions are unwrapped Cartesian, image-major, stride =3 * n_atoms=.

* Grow the band with AutoNEB
=howto/autoneb= is the AutoNEB loop: insert where the resolution is worst, relax a local window, and climb. The tuning sits on =BandConfig::autoneb=. A weighted spring supplied by the host is refused.

* Pick tangents, springs, projections
| Kind             | Variants                                                             |
|------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| =TangentKind=    | =Simple= (Mills–Jónsson–Schenter), =Improved= (Henkelman–Jónsson) |
| =SpringKind=     | =Uniform { k }=, =Weighted=, =EnergyWeighted=, =OnsagerMachlup=, =OnsagerMachlupLive= |
| =ProjectionKind= | =PlainElasticBand=, =Neb=, =DoublyNudged=                            |

Defaults: improved tangent, uniform spring =k = 5=, NEB projection, FIRE 2.0, and a climbing image that arms by =CiConfig::after(0.5)=, when the largest force component falls under 0.5. The armed climbing image is refined by its lowest mode (=BandConfig::min_mode=). =howto/band= has the references.

* Step one point with a stencil
=howto/stencil= builds =Stencil::point= for a minimiser, the dimer, Lanczos, ART, or eigen following. =Stencil::band= has no presets yet; its =build= returns the =BAND_PRESET_SEAM= error. The C names are =rgsaddle_stencil_*= and =rgsaddle_last_error=. Python is =rgsaddle.Stencil.point= and =rgsaddle.ase.Point=.

* Minimum-mode instead of a band
=MinModeSession= refreshes the lowest curvature mode (dimer rotation or Lanczos on finite-difference Hessian actions), inverts the force along it, and takes one solver step. Same host loop. =PointSurface= evals a single geometry.

A host that climbs with its own optimizer keeps one session for the whole search: =set_position= (=rgsaddle_minmode_set_position=) with the gradient it already has, then =estimate_mode= (=rgsaddle_minmode_estimate=). The previous mode seeds the next rotation, so a mode that still holds costs one evaluation. The dimer's Polak-Ribiere force and direction survive that move; =set_mode= and =reset= clear them, and a failed callback leaves them unchanged. Lanczos through the C estimate advances one Hessian action and keeps the Krylov basis until =set_position=, =set_mode=, or =reset=. The basis grows to the free dimension. After k actions the mode also takes its component on the residual direction, from the Lanczos polynomials at an extrapolated lowest eigenvalue, at no extra surface call; the reported curvature is the Ritz value of the k vectors. =MinModeSession::estimate_mode= and =step= still finish the residual test before a translation. A dimer estimate is the full rotation.

* Index-k minimum mode
=indexk_step= and =IndexKSession= reflect the force in the k lowest modes and take one translator step. =k = 1= is the dimer or Lanczos step, bit for bit. The block solver is LOBPCG or block Lanczos. See =howto/indexk=. From C, ABI minor 37, the names are =rgsaddle_indexk_*= and =rgsaddle_reflect_force=.

* Index-1 Newton
=Index1Session= takes a restricted-step partitioned rational function optimization (RFO) step on a dense Hessian and updates it with Powell or Bofill. Pass an initial Hessian, or leave it empty and the first step builds one by central differences. =Index1Config= is non-exhaustive. =with_sparse_pattern= builds that seed from a distance-2 colouring instead. The flag stays off unless set. The trust radius is the Euclidean bound on the Cartesian step. See @@rst::doc:`the sparse Hessian how-to <howto/sparse-hessian>`@@.

From C, =rgsaddle_index1_create= / =rgsaddle_index1_step= follow the same loop. The vector length is =3 * n_atoms=; a bead polymer passes =n_atoms * n_beads=. A host that owns the Hessian calls =rgsaddle_prfo_step=. =rgsaddle_nichols_step= is the i-PI shift on a spectrum (=nmode= may be smaller than =n= when external modes were dropped). =rgsaddle_hessian_bofill= or =rgsaddle_hessian_powell= updates the Hessian. The gradient on the wire is dE/dx.

* Do not keep solver history across a potential change
Call =reset= after any model update. That is the documented boundary. A host that skips it mixes two surfaces in one L-BFGS memory. =restart= is the optimizer-only reset for the same surface; it keeps the cached endpoint energies and the last evaluation.
