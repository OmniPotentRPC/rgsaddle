The C entry points convert atom, image, and dimension counts with a checked conversion and a checked product. A count that is negative, does not fit a ~size_t~, or would make a buffer past the largest slice now returns ~RGSADDLE_SHAPE~, ~RGSADDLE_INVALID_PARAMETER~, or a NULL session instead of truncating on a 32-bit target, and a negative ~max_rotations~, ~krylov_dim~, or ~max_calls~ is refused.
