IRC, Sella, constraint, SAMD, PES, internal coordinate, index-k, OCI-NEB, saddle-index, band, and minimum-mode entry points convert atom, image, bond, angle, dihedral, and dimension counts with a checked conversion and a checked product. A count that is negative, does not fit a ~size_t~, or would make a buffer past the largest slice returns ~RGSADDLE_SHAPE~, ~RGSADDLE_INVALID_PARAMETER~, or a NULL session instead of truncating on a 32 bit target. A negative ~max_rotations~, ~krylov_dim~, or ~max_calls~ is refused.
