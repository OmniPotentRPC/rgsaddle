A PAFI plane sampled with the windowed chain scales the correction term =ψ= with the parameter of the path. A path with a tangent of length other than one, and no rigid directions to drop, took the stored tangent and curvature as they were, so rescaling the parameter by =k= changed =ψ= by =k²=. The plane now takes its unit normal, =τ · ν= and =dν/ds= from the stored tangent whatever the directions, and the mean =ψ= does not depend on the parameter.
