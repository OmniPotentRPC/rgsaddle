=DirectionCover= chooses the saddle searches out of one minimum for adaptive kinetic Monte Carlo callers. Every active atom is its own centre with an even signed cover of unit vectors on its sphere and an optional Gaussian kick on its neighbours. Each arm runs as one =ArtSession=. The gap policy orders untried arms by their angular gap and the distinct states around it, the start included, and the expected-improvement policy scores the barrier with a Gaussian process on each sphere. =coverage= reports the exact covering radius per centre, a record per arm, distinct saddles against draws with their frequency counts, and the next arm with its score. Records do not depend on the thread count. No Hessian is built. Xu and Henkelman, J. Chem. Phys. 129, 114104 (2008), doi:10.1063/1.2976010.
