The PAFI barrier of the isolated translation no longer depends on the frame its band is built in or the way the band is traversed. At 300 K the earlier estimator gave 86.2±5.0 meV in the frame of the campaign and 95.6 and 95.5 meV in two others, and the causes were in the method. The plane of a frame was normal to the chord into it, so a band traversed the other way had other planes, and each plane was tilted by half the angle the path turned over that chord, which moves the thermal part of the barrier at first order. The trapezoid of the full gradient over thirteen frames missed the zero-temperature barrier of 90.37 meV by 2 to 6 meV, by an amount that depended on the normal (84.3, 88.6 and 87.6 meV with the incoming chord, the outgoing chord and their bisector). Near the saddle the planes also cut the basin of an interstitial 0.9 Å away and up to 85 meV below the minimum of the plane; a chain reached it within tens of time units and then averaged over it. Normals on every atom let the elastic far field take up the projection while the defect slid back. Chains also started cold, the Euler-Maruyama step widened the wells at first order in the step, and block means of short correlated series gave errors that were too small. =pafi_frames= with the jump test removes each of these.
=benchmarks/defect_pair_pafi_frames.csv= rates the isolated translation at 300 K in the 13³ cell on the as-built band, on the same band traversed the other way, and on the bands to (3,-1,0) and (3,0,1), at overdamped steps of 0.1 and 0.2 with a mobility of 0.2, on the 340 atoms that carry 95 % of the summed squared steps. The forward barriers are 77.1 to 85.3 meV at 0.1, a mean of 82.0±2.3 meV with χ² 1.4 on 3 degrees of freedom, and 78.2 to 95.5 meV at 0.2, a mean of 84.8±2.3 meV with χ² 9.9 on 3. A second seed gives 83.1±5.6 meV, and all nine agree with χ² 12.1 on 8 about 83.4±1.6 meV. The minima of the planes put the zero-temperature barrier at 92.1 meV. =pafi_frames_agree_across_frames= checks the file, and =defect_pair_pafi_frames_planes.csv= holds every plane. The campaign uses its own frame, like every class: 80.2±4.6 meV at 300 K. Planes on the 65 atoms that carry 90 % of the steps, and on the 2678 that carry 99 %, give 84.2±4.8 and 73.4±4.7 meV with the same noise as the campaign frame, so the barrier falls as a plane takes in more of the far field. With every near-shell class and both isolated hops rated again this way, the 30 Å cell-decay V_iv is 202.0±20.6, 194.7±18.9 and 164.4±25.5 Ω at 300, 500 and 700 K, where the earlier estimator gave 228.2±22.5, 194.0±20.3 and 206.4±19.6 Ω.
