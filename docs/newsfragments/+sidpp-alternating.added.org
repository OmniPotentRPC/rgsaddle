=sidpp_alternating_path= is the sequential IDPP with the alternating growth rule that eOn and GPRidge use: a new image at a fixed fraction of the open gap, a collective relaxation, and equal arc-length placement at the end. =sidpp_alternating_inverse_distance_path= is that growth with inverse-distance pair targets. =idpp_path_fixed= is =idpp_path= with a frozen-atom mask. =PathInitializer= gains =Sidpp= and =SidppAlternating=. The C ABI, minor 71, exports =rgsaddle_idpp_fixed_path=, =rgsaddle_sidpp_alternating_path= and =rgsaddle_sidpp_alternating_inverse_distance_path=.
