PAFI rates now have a system-agnostic Bennett--Chandler correction:
=pafi_transmission= shoots constrained-equilibrium samples retained on the
maximum-free-energy plane in both time directions and reports =κ=, its
block error and shot counts.  =defect_pair_rates= and the V_iv near-shell
ledger apply the same class =κ= to both directions and propagate
=(σ_κ/κ)²= in the logarithmic rate draws.  The C ABI exposes the same
operation as =rgsaddle_pafi_transmission= (ABI minor 46).

A 32-shot 300 K smoke calculation is recorded in
=benchmarks/defect_pair_kappa_smoke.csv= for the isolated translation and
the three highest-sensitivity near-shell classes.  These short estimates
are diagnostics for BenchBot's full campaign, not converged rate data.
