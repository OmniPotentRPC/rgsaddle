~rgsaddle.ase.NEBOptimizer~ drives an ASE band with the adaptive ODE12r step (~method='ODE'~) or a fixed step (~method='static'~).
The stop is ~NEB.get_residual~.
~get_forces~ stores that residual as the infinity norm of the projected force on each interior image, and ~get_residual~ returns the largest of those norms.
A call to ~get_residual~ before ~get_forces~ raises ~RuntimeError~.
The climbing image is left out of the maximum only when one was chosen.
On an exact tie of interior energies the omitted image is the first maximum, while ASE omits ~1 + argsort~ of the interior energies.
