An inner trial of the intrinsic reaction coordinate (IRC) finds the level shift that puts it on the sphere by bisection, so the trial is the minimum of the quasi-Newton model on the sphere at every radius.
Before, rgmin's ~qn_irc_restricted~ began a Newton search at a shift of zero.
A model eigenvalue zero to round-off puts a pole there.
From that pole a Newton iterate can at most double the shift plus the denominator floor of 1e-16, and the search stops after 64 of them, so a shift above about 922 is out of reach.
The radial projection of its last trial is then not the minimum of the model on the sphere.

The largest distance of those points to the path was 3.7097e-3, above the 3.4182e-3 at radius 0.0125.
The branch now never halves its radius, and the distance over 0.4 of arc is 1.7208e-3.
