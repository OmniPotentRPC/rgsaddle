~LandscapeSession~ searches a solution landscape downward from a certified saddle. It pushes along each unstable eigenvector and follows index-(k-1) minimum-mode dynamics, the high-index saddle step of Yin, Zhang, and Zhang, SIAM J. Sci. Comput. 41, A3576 (2019), until a critical point of index one less, and repeats down to minima with an rgmin solve on the true gradient. The result is a ~PathwayMap~ of ~CriticalPoint~ nodes and ~LandscapeEdge~ connections, the pathway construction of Yin, Wang, Chen, Zhang, and Zhang, Phys. Rev. Lett. 124, 090601 (2020).
~LandscapeConfig~ sets the duplicate distance, the push length, and hard caps on nodes and force evaluations. Reaching a cap is ~SaddleError::LandscapeBound~ (~LandscapeBoundKind~) with the counts, and the partial pathway is not returned. Distances use ~Cell::minimum_image~ when a cell is set.
On the separable well, with an analytic Hessian action, push 0.5 and force tolerance 1e-8, the map from the origin stored 9 nodes and 12 edges in 865 force evaluations. The parent cost 1, the two index-1 saddles on the x axis cost 7 each, the two on the y axis cost 43 each, and each of the four minima cost 79. The other 448 evaluations are later edges into nodes already stored.
On Müller-Brown, with an analytic action, push 0.2 and force tolerance 1e-6, the map from the saddle at (-0.822001558733, 0.624312802815) stored 3 nodes in 219 force evaluations (1, then 131 and 87), and the map from (0.212486582001, 0.292988325107) stored 3 nodes in 201 (1, then 114 and 86).
The C ABI adds ~rgsaddle_landscape_config_default~, ~rgsaddle_landscape_create~, ~rgsaddle_landscape_run~, ~rgsaddle_landscape_node~, ~rgsaddle_landscape_modes~, ~rgsaddle_landscape_edge~, ~rgsaddle_landscape_free~, ~rgsaddle_hessian_at_fn~, ~rgsaddle_indexk_step_at~, and ~RGSADDLE_LANDSCAPE_BOUND~. ~rgsaddle_hessian_fn~ keeps its original signature.
The C++ owner is ~rgsaddle::Landscape~. Its ~run~ raises ~std::runtime_error~ when the search reaches a node or evaluation limit. Python adds ~Landscape~, ~PathwayMap~, ~CriticalPoint~, and ~LandscapeEdge~. ~PathwayMap.nodes~ and ~PathwayMap.edges~ are typed lists. The Hessian callback receives the geometry and the vector. The index-k leg uses ~LandscapeConfig::force_gate~, and a minimum stops on ~RelaxConfig::force_tol~.
