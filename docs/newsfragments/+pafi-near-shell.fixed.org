The near-shell rates of the copper V_iv that carry its weight are now PAFI free-energy rates, not the barrier-shift model. =benchmarks/defect_pair_network_sensitivity.csv= ranks every hop class inside the census radius by =dV/d ln k= of the 30 Å cell-decay volume, by central differences of 0.1 in =ln k= on both directions of the class. Classes are taken in that order until the rest, all moved by the model spread together, shift the volume by less than the smaller Table 1 error (21, 15 and 10 Ω at 300, 500 and 700 K). With the spread of 0.576 in =ln k= from the eight recorded PAFI rows that selects 18, 27 and 35 of 162, 166 and 166 classes. After they were rated the cover was repeated with the spread of the model against them, and at 300 K it called for 4 and then 2 more. The largest sensitivities, 8.8, 10.3 and 10.4 Ω per unit =ln k=, are translations into the spontaneous (3,3,0) state.
=benchmarks/defect_pair_network_pafi.csv= holds the rates: 24, 27 and 35 hop classes and the isolated translation and rotation at each temperature, all rated, in the 13³ cell on the Table 1 lattices. Each class's saddle is searched again as in the network campaign. A climbing band of thirteen images with energy-weighted springs joins the bound state, the verified dimer saddle (the band top for one class at 300 and one at 500 K) and the relaxed target basin; a band into recombination ends 0.3 Å past the saddle. Bands are kept at a force of at most 0.01 eV/Å. PAFI samples each image's hyperplane until the barrier error is at most 0.2 kT, or four passes. One path gives both directions: the forward barrier above the reactant and =FreeEnergyProfile::reverse_barrier= above the last plane, each with the attempt frequency from the curvature at its end. A direction runs at =k_kind (nu/nu_iso) exp(-(F - F_iso)/kT)= against the isolated hop of the same kind. That hop ends in a state equivalent to its start: its two directions differ by 0.013, 0.017 and 0.030 eV for the translation and 0.002, 0.021 and 0.000 eV for the rotation, so =F_iso= is their inverse-variance mean, and half the gap is added in quadrature to the isolated error and to every direction of that kind. On the bound-to-bound paths the same gap less the state energies averages -0.013, -0.012 and +0.006 eV, so it is a per-path drift, not a shift common to every path. Against the 43, 46 and 59 rated directions the model's =ln k= is off by 0.708, 0.515 and 0.411 in root mean square, and too fast by 0.37 and 0.39 on average at 300 and 500 K.

Each PAFI direction draws within its own error and each isolated kind within its own.
