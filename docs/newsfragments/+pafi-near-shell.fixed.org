The near-shell rates of the copper V_iv that carry its weight are now PAFI free-energy rates, not the barrier-shift model. =benchmarks/defect_pair_network_sensitivity.csv= ranks every hop class inside the census radius by =dV/d ln k= of the 30 Å cell-decay volume, by central differences of 0.1 in =ln k= on both directions of the class. Classes are taken in that order until the rest, all moved by the model spread together, shift the volume by less than the smaller Table 1 error (21, 15 and 10 Ω at 300, 500 and 700 K). With the spread of 0.576 in =ln k= from the eight recorded PAFI rows that selects 18, 27 and 35 of 162, 166 and 166 classes. Once they are rated the cover is repeated on the classes still on the model, with the spread of the calibrated model below and half the Table 1 error. Earlier passes with the uncalibrated spread called for 4 and then 2 more at 300 K, and one during the calibration fit for 3 more at 700 K; the 13 unrated 300 K classes ranked next were rated as well, to test the coverage, and the calibrated cover of the earlier rates called for 10 more at 700 K. With 37, 27 and 48 classes rated the rest shift the decay volume by at most 3.27, 3.53 and 4.31 Ω against 10.5, 7.5 and 5 Ω, and the cover selects no more. The largest sensitivities, 8.8, 10.3 and 10.4 Ω per unit =ln k=, are translations into the spontaneous (3,3,0) state; on the rated network every class moved together moves the decay volume by 102.9, 103.2 and 99.7 Ω per unit =ln k=.
=benchmarks/defect_pair_network_pafi.csv= holds the rates: 37, 27 and 48 hop classes and the isolated translation and rotation at each temperature, all rated, in the 13³ cell on the Table 1 lattices. Each class's saddle is searched again as in the network campaign. A climbing band of thirteen images with energy-weighted springs joins the bound state, the verified dimer saddle (the band top for one class at 300 and one at 500 K) and the relaxed target basin; a band into recombination ends 0.3 Å past the saddle. Bands are kept at a force of at most 0.01 eV/Å. =pafi_frames= samples a plane on every frame of the band with the jump test, as for the isolated translation in =benchmarks/defect_pair_pafi_frames.csv= at the step of 0.2, until the barrier error is at most 0.2 kT, or four passes. One path gives both directions: the forward barrier above the reactant and =FreeEnergyProfile::reverse_barrier= above the last plane, each with the attempt frequency from the curvature at its end. A direction runs at =k_kind (nu/nu_iso) exp(-(F - F_iso)/kT)= against the isolated hop of the same kind. The two barriers of a path should differ by the gap of its ends. The isolated hop ends in a state equivalent to its start, and its forward barrier exceeds the back one by -0.0048, +0.0119 and -0.0011 eV translating and by -0.0236, -0.0394 and -0.0373 eV rotating; on the 31, 19 and 37 classes rated both ways the forward less the reverse barrier drifts from the zero-temperature gap of the ends by +0.018, +0.154 and -0.244 kT on average and 0.675, 0.617 and 0.605 kT in root mean square, a χ² of 36, 33 and 68 against their errors, where the earlier estimator gave 168, 79 and 135. With that estimator four 300 K classes integrated again from the other end placed the drift past the dividing surface: the barriers read from the start of each integration agreed, and those read from its end did not. =anchored_barriers= therefore keeps the forward barrier of each path and its error and puts the reverse barrier the zero-temperature gap below it, and the isolated hops take their forward barriers, so that a class and its reference are read alike. The 6, 8 and 11 recombinations are rated one way only and keep their forward error. Against the 37, 27 and 48 rated classes, one residual each from the mean over its directions, the barrier-shift model's =ln k= is off by 0.518, 0.372 and 0.440 in root mean square and by -0.1155, -0.0243 and -0.1370 on average, computed less model. =calibrate_surrogate= fits that bias by weighted least squares with a Paule–Mandel scatter, -0.1902±0.0449, -0.0730±0.0458 and -0.1420±0.0602 with a scatter of 0.1994, 0.1303 and 0.3688, and every direction still on the model runs at =exp(c)= times its model rate, both directions of a class alike.
