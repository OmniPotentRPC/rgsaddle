The near-shell rates of the copper V_iv that carry its weight are now PAFI free-energy rates, not the barrier-shift model. =benchmarks/defect_pair_network_sensitivity.csv= ranks every hop class inside the census radius by =dV/d ln k= of the 30 Å cell-decay volume, by central differences of 0.1 in =ln k= on both directions of the class. Classes are taken in that order until the rest, all moved by the model spread together, shift the volume by less than the smaller Table 1 error (21, 15 and 10 Ω at 300, 500 and 700 K). With the spread of 0.576 in =ln k= from the eight recorded PAFI rows that selects 18, 27 and 35 of 162, 166 and 166 classes. Once they are rated the cover is repeated on the classes still on the model, with the spread of the calibrated model below and half the Table 1 error. Earlier passes with the uncalibrated spread called for 4 and then 2 more at 300 K, and one during the calibration fit for 3 more at 700 K; the 13 unrated 300 K classes ranked next were rated as well, to test the coverage. With 37, 27 and 38 classes rated the rest shift the decay volume by at most 1.88, 1.62 and 4.86 Ω against 10.5, 7.5 and 5 Ω, and the cover selects none. The largest sensitivities, 8.8, 10.3 and 10.4 Ω per unit =ln k=, are translations into the spontaneous (3,3,0) state; on the rated network every class moved together moves the decay volume by 99.9, 98.1 and 102.9 Ω per unit =ln k=.
=benchmarks/defect_pair_network_pafi.csv= holds the rates: 37, 27 and 38 hop classes and the isolated translation and rotation at each temperature, all rated, in the 13³ cell on the Table 1 lattices. Each class's saddle is searched again as in the network campaign. A climbing band of thirteen images with energy-weighted springs joins the bound state, the verified dimer saddle (the band top for one class at 300 and one at 500 K) and the relaxed target basin; a band into recombination ends 0.3 Å past the saddle. Bands are kept at a force of at most 0.01 eV/Å. PAFI samples each image's hyperplane until the barrier error is at most 0.2 kT, or four passes. One path gives both directions: the forward barrier above the reactant and =FreeEnergyProfile::reverse_barrier= above the last plane, each with the attempt frequency from the curvature at its end. A direction runs at =k_kind (nu/nu_iso) exp(-(F - F_iso)/kT)= against the isolated hop of the same kind. The two barriers of a path should differ by the gap of its ends, but the integration drifts from it. The isolated hop ends in a state equivalent to its start, yet its directions differ by 0.013, 0.017 and 0.030 eV translating and 0.002, 0.021 and 0.000 eV rotating; on the 31, 19 and 27 classes rated both ways the forward less the reverse barrier drifts from the zero-temperature gap of the ends by -0.460, -0.282 and +0.078 kT on average and 0.776, 0.528 and 0.513 kT in root mean square, a χ² of 168, 80 and 135 against their errors. =balanced_barriers= takes the inverse-variance mean of the forward barrier and the reverse barrier plus the gap, no gap for the isolated hop, and adds half the drift to its error in quadrature. The 6, 8 and 11 recombinations are rated one way only and take half the isolated gap of their kind in quadrature. Against the 37, 27 and 38 rated classes, one residual each from the mean over its directions, the barrier-shift model's =ln k= is off by 0.568, 0.434 and 0.357 in root mean square and too fast by 0.394, 0.375 and 0.022 on average. =calibrate_surrogate= fits that bias by weighted least squares with a Paule–Mandel scatter, -0.4821±0.0445, -0.3994±0.0413 and -0.0433±0.0628 with a scatter of 0.1419, 0.0595 and 0.3020, and every direction still on the model runs at =exp(c)= times its model rate, both directions of a class alike. The bias holds out to the census radius: on the classes whose outer end lies 9 to 11 Å out the weighted mean residual is -0.41, -0.35 and -0.01.
