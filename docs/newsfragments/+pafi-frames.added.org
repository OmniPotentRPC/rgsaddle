=pafi_frames= samples one PAFI plane on every frame of a band, with planes from =ArcPath::frame_planes=. A frame's normal is the unit tangent of the parabola through it and its two neighbours in chord arc length, and its =dn/ds= the same derivative of the normals (=FramePlanes=). Both are second order in the spacing, a reversed band gives the same planes, and they act on the fewest atoms that carry =PafiConfig::active_share= of the summed squared steps. Each frame is relaxed in its plane to =relax_tol=, its chain starts at that minimum =x*_k=, and with =g = (∇E · n)(1 - ψ)= the profile is =F_k = E*_k - E*_0= plus the trapezoid of =⟨g⟩ - g(x*_k)=, so the zero-temperature part holds at any spacing. =PafiConfig::max_jump=, =jump_tol= and =window= are the =MaxJumpThresh= test of Swinburne's PAFI: every =window= samples the chain is relaxed in its plane, and when an atom ends further than =max_jump= from the minimum of the plane, in the basin of another state that the plane also cuts, those samples are dropped and the chain goes back to where the last kept window ended. =HyperplaneAverage= now records the energy and =∇E · n= where its chain starts, the widest atom excursion, and the dropped windows. =Integrator= selects the overdamped step.
