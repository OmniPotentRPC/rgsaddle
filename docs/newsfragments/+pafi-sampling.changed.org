=HyperplaneAverage= is =#[non_exhaustive]=, like the new =FramePlanes= and =Integrator=. The default step is =Integrator::LeimkuhlerMatthews=, which samples a harmonic well exactly at any stable step, and =Integrator::EulerMaruyama= is the earlier one, whose averages carry a bias of first order in the step. A plane that needs more samples continues its chain instead of starting again, and its standard error is the larger of the block-mean error and =sqrt(2 τ var / n)=, with =τ= the integrated autocorrelation time in a Sokal window; the OH-TST estimators keep their block errors. =pafi_profile= and =defect_pair_rates= therefore give other numbers than before for the same seed.
