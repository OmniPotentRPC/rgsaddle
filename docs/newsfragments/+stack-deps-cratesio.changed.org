The ~minimage~ dependency now resolves from crates.io at ~=0.2.3~ with ~capi-compat-names~ disabled, so its C ABI exports use the ~minimage_*~ prefix and do not define ~mi_version~ when linked into binaries alongside ~readcon-core~ and ~mimalloc~.
The ~linkcell~ dependency in ~Cargo.toml~ and ~ci/consumer/Cargo.toml~ now resolves from crates.io at ~=0.4.2~ instead of a pinned git revision, ~SparsityPattern::from_positions~ uses ~linkcell::pairs_within~ and ~linkcell::pairs_open~, and ~readcon-core~ stays at ~=0.17.1~ to share one version with ~readcon-db~ ~0.2.1~.
~Cell::as_minimage~, ~Cell::minimum_image~, and ~mic::wrap_difference~ delegate to ~minimage::Cell::displacement~, which wraps orthorhombic and triclinic separations past 1.5 box lengths (such as 25 and 35 in a box of 10 to a distance of 5) across every periodic image.
