~BandSession::set_image_threads(threads)~ splits each band evaluation
into contiguous image blocks on a pool the session keeps.
One thread stays the default; zero threads is refused.
On a surface whose rows are independent the trajectory is bitwise that
of one thread.
With 16 interior images of 512 Lennard-Jones atoms, 8 threads run a band
step 4.36 times as fast as the parent; =howto/band= gives the strong and
weak scaling and what limits it.
~examples/band_scaling.rs~ and ~docs/source/figures/band-scaling~ time
the runs and draw the figures.
