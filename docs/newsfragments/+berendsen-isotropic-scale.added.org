=berendsen_isotropic_scale= is one isotropic Berendsen step on the hydrostatic tension from =finite_difference_cauchy_stress=.
=BerendsenIsotropic= is non-exhaustive.
Start from =Default= and use =with_max_strain_per_step= to cap the absolute change of the volume factor.
The caller supplies that tension, and the function does not add the kinetic pressure.
The scale multiplies every cell vector and every Cartesian position, including the entries off the diagonal of a triclinic cell.
Berendsen, Postma, van Gunsteren, DiNola, and Haak, J. Chem. Phys. 81, 3684 (1984), doi:10.1063/1.448118.
