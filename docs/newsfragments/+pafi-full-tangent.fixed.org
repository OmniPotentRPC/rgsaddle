=pafi_frames= constrains the full path tangent.

A masked normal is a different plane, and that made the barrier depend on the share. The chain samples the Euclidean measure on =(x - X) · ν = 0=. With =τ = dX/dr= and =ν= the unit constraint normal, that free energy has

: dF/dr = ⟨ (∇E · ν) (τ · ν - (x - X) · dν/dr) ⟩.

When =ν= is the unit tangent and =r= its arc length, =τ · ν = 1= and this is the multiply form LAMMPS =fix pafi= averages, =⟨(∇E · n)(1 - ψ)⟩= (Swinburne and Marinica, Phys. Rev. Lett. 120, 135503 (2018), doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.120.135503). Truncating =τ= and renormalizing gives a different =ν=. On a harmonic transverse well the two barriers differ by =(kT/2) ln[(c_s(τ)/c_s(ν))(c_m(ν)/c_m(τ))]=, with =c(n) = nᵀ H⁻¹ n= from =det(H restricted to n⊥) = det(H) c(n)=. =harmonic_mask_shift= is that map. It assumes both planes pass through the same points. A masked normal that is off the force direction lets the minimum slide off the path, and the energy of that slide is =E(path) - E(minimum)=.

A component of the normal along a rigid translation couples the plane to a uniform drift, so =HyperplaneConstraint::orthogonal_to= removes those directions by modified Gram-Schmidt and drops a row only inside Higham's =γ_d= roundoff bound. The estimator uses the same unit normal. The integrated autocorrelation time widens the error bar when more atoms are constrained and leaves the mean unchanged.

=pafi_truncated= builds the old truncated planes. The rows already in =benchmarks/defect_pair_pafi_frames.csv= are that coordinate, including 84.2±4.8 meV on 65 atoms, 80.2±4.6 meV on 340, and 73.4±4.7 meV on 2678. Those volumes keep their earlier ratings.
