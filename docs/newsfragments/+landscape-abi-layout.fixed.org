~rgsaddle_hessian_fn~ keeps its original four-argument signature, so ~rgsaddle_indexk_step~ and ~rgsaddle_kappa_dimer_force~ are unchanged. The geometry-aware Hessian action is the new ~rgsaddle_hessian_at_fn~, taken by ~rgsaddle_indexk_step_at~, ~rgsaddle_landscape_run~, ~rgsaddle::IndexK::step_at~, and ~rgsaddle::Landscape::run~. ~rgsaddle_landscape_report_t~ now carries ~uint64_t flags~ right after ~version~, written as zero, so its size is 48 bytes and ~n_nodes~, ~n_edges~, ~bound~, and ~evaluations~ sit at offsets 16, 24, 32, and 40. The ABI contract guards pin the original callback type and the new one independently. The new symbols and the report layout arrive with ABI minor 68.
