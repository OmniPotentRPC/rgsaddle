=DirectionCover= expected improvement now uses a Gaussian kernel of the chordal distance between directions, which is positive definite on the sphere for every =length_scale=. The geodesic Gaussian it replaces is not, and at =length_scale= 2 its factorization hit negative pivots that were silently clamped, which drove the posterior variance negative and the expected improvement to the bare mean gain.
